# Iran Hypersonic Salvos Strike Ramat David

Iran has launched a three-wave hypersonic ballistic missile strike targeting military installations across Israel, including Ramat David Airbase. The strike was executed in fulfillment of deterrent promises made by the Khatam al-Anbiya headquarters following Israeli airstrikes on the Dahiyeh district of Beirut. The escalation has exposed strategic differences between Washington and Tel Aviv, prompting emergency airspace closures across the Gulf.

## Retaliatory Salvos: Three-Wave Hypersonic Strike Hits Israel

Tehran has executed high-velocity ballistic missile strikes targeting military sectors:
*   **Three Waves**: The attack consisted of three separate hypersonic ballistic missile salvos.
*   **The Targets**: Munitions targeted defense installations in northern and central districts.
*   **Penetration**: The hypersonic missiles successfully bypassed active regional defense screens.

---

## Targeting Ramat David: Direct Impact on Northern Aviation Base

The primary focus of the missile strikes was the air support base in the northern sector:
*   **Ramat David**: The strategic Ramat David Airbase suffered multiple direct impacts.
*   **Base Damage**: Runways and aircraft shelter infrastructure sustained structural damage.
*   **Operational Status**: Flight operations were suspended to allow safety checks and repairs.

---

## The Information Blackout: Tel Aviv Bans Strike Site Media

Israeli authorities implemented immediate information controls following the strikes:
*   **The Blackout**: A strict ban was placed on taking or publishing photos of the hit locations.
*   **Legal Action**: Citizens were warned that publishing strike footage would result in prosecution.
*   **Damage Control**: The measure is intended to prevent adversary teams from assessing strike accuracy.

---

## Coordination Rift: Donald Trump Condemns Beirut Bombings

A diplomatic split has emerged between the U.S. President and the Israeli cabinet:
*   **Trump's Stance**: Trump stated to *Fox News* that he was not satisfied with the Beirut strikes.
*   **No Coordination**: The President confirmed that Tel Aviv did not coordinate the attack with Washington.
*   **Netanyahu Call**: Reports suggest Trump contacted Netanyahu to demand a halt to unilateral actions.

---

## Trump's Message to Tehran: Return to the Negotiation Table

The U.S. administration has signaled a desire to close the current escalation loop:
*   **The Appeal**: Trump publically urged Tehran to view the strikes as completed: "You have fired, now return."
*   **Negotiation Push**: The President urged both sides to focus on drafting the final treaty terms.
*   **Military Restraint**: The statements indicate Washington seeks to avoid being drawn into a broader war.

---

## The Deterrent Equation: Khatam al-Anbiya's Dahiyeh Warning

Iranian commanders have defined the security parameters for regional capitals:
*   **The Equation**: The Khatam al-Anbiya command confirmed the strikes were triggered by Beirut bombings.
*   **Deterrence Rules**: Iranian officials warned that strikes on Beirut would result in direct strikes on Israel.
*   **State Conflict**: The exchange was managed as a direct confrontation, separate from local border clashes.

---

## Regional Flight Rerouting: Qatar Closes Airspace Under NOTAM

The scale of the ballistic salvos triggered emergency aviation warnings in the Gulf:
*   **Airspace Closure**: Qatar's Civil Aviation Authority issued emergency NOTAM A082/26.
*   **Flight Rerouting**: Flights departing from Doha and Saudi Arabia were rerouted to safe paths.
*   **Radar Alerts**: The measures followed radar alerts of missile paths crossing regional corridors.

---

## Escalation Threats: Ben-Gvir Demands Strikes on Tehran

Extreme elements in the Israeli cabinet have called for immediate military options:
*   **Ben-Gvir's Call**: Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir demanded that Tel Aviv execute strikes on Tehran.
*   **Cabinet Dispute**: Moderate officials warned that direct strikes would lead to full regional war.
*   **Security Meeting**: The security cabinet met in emergency session to draft response options.

## Conclusion: The Operational Reality of State-to-State Parity

The hypersonic missile strikes on Ramat David Airbase and the subsequent airspace closures show the operational reality of regional deterrence.

By executing a three-wave hypersonic strike in response to Beirut bombings, Iran has demonstrated its capacity to enforce its deterrent equations. Donald Trump's public disagreement with the Beirut operations and his calls for negotiation show the strategic limits of U.S. support for unilateral actions. As Israel maintains its information blackout and Qatar reroutes flights, these developments confirm that future security arrangements will be defined by direct, state-to-state parity.
