# Abqaiq Refinery Strike: Jizan Shutdown

Saudi Aramco has shut down its 400,000 barrel-per-day Jizan refinery and halted major units at the Abqaiq oil processing complex. The closures, confirmed by technical consulting memos from IIR, follow precise Houthi drone and missile strikes. Despite official claims of complete interceptions, satellite imagery confirms damage to storage installations, highlighting the accuracy of Houthi strike systems.

## Jizan Refinery Closed: Technical Shut Down Following Strikes

The strike on Jizan forced the immediate suspension of refinery operations:
*   **The Closure**: Aramco shut the 400,000 bpd Jizan refinery on July 27.
*   **Technical Memo**: IIR consulting reports confirmed the shutdown was due to damage.
*   **Production Loss**: The halt affects refined product supplies for the southern provinces.

---

## Abqaiq Complex Hit: Houthi Drones Penetrate Defenses

The world's largest crude processing facility suffered direct hits to key units:
*   **The Attack**: Houthi drones targeted processing towers at the Abqaiq complex.
*   **Shutdown Order**: Operators deactivated the largest processing units to prevent fires.
*   **System Shock**: The hit disrupted intermediate processing stages for light crude.

## Cooling Covers Installed: Saudi Efforts to Limit Tank Damage

Satellite imagery revealed active damage control measures at the Abqaiq site:
*   **Cooling Covers**: Engineers installed black and white covers on two gas storage tanks.
*   **Thermal Control**: The covers aim to limit heat transfer to adjacent fuel silos.
*   **Damage Assessment**: The visible measures confirm the failure of short-range defenses.

## Yanbu Interceptions: The Limits of Patriot PAC-3 Defenses

Joint Greek-Saudi defense units in Yanbu reported limited success against incoming targets:
*   **The Intercept**: Defense batteries engaged only two ballistic missiles at 7:15 PM.
*   **Drone Arrivals**: An additional drone hit the perimeter at 11:07 PM without engagement.
*   **Battery Status**: The Patriot PAC-3 batteries failed to track the final maneuver paths.

## Oil Supply Disruptions: The Threat to Global Energy Markets

The closures at Jizan and Abqaiq have affected regional oil logistics:
*   **Logistics Freeze**: Crude flows from eastern fields to Red Sea ports are restricted.
*   **Market Impact**: Shipping delays have increased cargo insurance rates in the Gulf.
*   **Export Deficit**: The halts restrict Saudi Arabia's ability to maintain export targets.

## Conclusion: The Vulnerability of Centralized Refining Networks

The closure of the Jizan refinery and the strikes on the Abqaiq complex show the vulnerability of centralized refining networks.

By executing precise drone strikes on processing units and gas storage tanks, Houthi forces have challenged Saudi air defense capabilities. The installation of thermal cooling covers and the Jizan shutdown confirm that critical energy nodes remain vulnerable. As Aramco engineers repair the damaged units, these developments show that refining infrastructure cannot be fully protected by air defenses during high-intensity campaigns.
