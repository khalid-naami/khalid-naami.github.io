# Iran Reverse Engineers US JASSM-ER Missile

A strategic analysis of drone losses indicates that the downing of 28 U.S. MQ-9 Reaper platforms over Yemen has cost the Pentagon over half a billion dollars. Concurrently, Israeli intelligence reports confirm that Iranian defense research centers have recovered a mostly intact U.S. AGM-158B JASSM-ER stealth cruise missile near Arak. The capture has initiated a joint reverse-engineering effort to analyze Western stealth technologies.

## The Cost of Containment: Half a Billion Lost in MQ-9 Downings

The financial impact of unmanned aircraft losses has reached a significant scale:
*   **The Cost**: The loss of 28 MQ-9 Reaper drones over Yemen has cost over $500 million.
*   **Unit Price**: Each unit represents an investment of $30 million to $34 million.
*   **Loss Frequency**: In early 2025, seven platforms were shot down in a six-week period.

## The Arak Capture: Wreckage of U.S. AGM-158B JASSM-ER Recovered

The recovery of a high-technology stealth cruise missile has challenged security parameters:
*   **The Capture**: An AGM-158B JASSM-ER missile crashed and was recovered near Arak in central Iran.
*   **Wreckage Quality**: The main structural sections and guidance packages remained intact.
*   **Research Transfer**: The wreckage was transferred to defense laboratories in Tehran for analysis.

## Reverse Engineering Protocols: Analyzing Stealth Composite Materials

Material scientists are focusing on the composition of the missile's outer shell:
*   **Stealth Composites**: Analysts are reviewing the composite materials that absorb radar waves.
*   **Coating Layers**: The study aims to identify the structure of radar-absorbent coatings.
*   **Manufacturing Methods**: Replicating these materials would allow Iran to improve its own stealth designs.

## Aerodynamic Reconstruction: Replicating Radar-Absorbent Structures

The physical design of the fuselage provides data on radar signature management:
*   **Aerodynamics**: Researchers are measuring the angles and joint tolerances of the wings.
*   **Signature Reduction**: The design shows how the U.S. combines fuel capacity with radar signature reduction.
*   **Modeling**: Wind tunnel tests are used to create digital models of the missile's flight profile.

## Guidance Systems Decryption: Deciphering INS and GPS Interoperability

The electronics package contains advanced navigation and target recognition systems:
*   **Guidance Decryption**: Analysts seek to access the INS/GPS flight computer architecture.
*   **Antenna Design**: The GPS antennas are studied for resistance to electronic jamming.
*   **Target Selection**: The terminal infrared sensor provides data on automated target selection algorithms.

## Propulsion Analysis: Turbojet Diagnostics and Thermal Signature Limits

The propulsion compartment contains a compact turbojet engine optimized for long range:
*   **Turbojet Study**: The study targets the materials used in the engine's turbine blades.
*   **Fuel Efficiency**: Replicating the engine's fuel efficiency would expand the range of local designs.
*   **Thermal Signature**: The exhaust nozzle shape shows how the system reduces its thermal signature.
