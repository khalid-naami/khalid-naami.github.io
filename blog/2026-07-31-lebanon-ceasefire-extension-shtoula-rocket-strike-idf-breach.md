# Lebanon Ceasefire Extension: Shtoula Strike

The fragile truce along the Lebanon-Israel border is facing challenges as U.S. President Donald Trump proposes a three-week extension of the ceasefire. The proposal comes in the wake of continued Israeli demolitions in southern border villages, which prompted a retaliatory rocket strike by Hezbollah on an IDF gathering in Shtoula. The developments highlight the difficulties of establishing a long-term agreement under threat of renewed air strikes.

## ## Trump's Three-Week Extension: A Strategic Timeout

The U.S. administration is seeking additional time to resolve the border dispute:
*   **The Proposal**: Trump suggested extending the active ceasefire by 21 days to facilitate direct talks.
*   **Preventing Collapse**: The extension seeks to prevent a return to full hostilities when the current 10-day truce expires.
*   **The Negotiation Window**: The additional three weeks would allow mediators to refine security arrangements.

---

## ## Yellow Zone Demolitions: The Destruction of Fifty-Five Villages

Lebanese officials have accused the IDF of systematic violations of the truce terms:
*   **Yellow Zone Doctrine**: Israel has executed controlled demolitions of civilian facilities under the pretext of clearing rocket launch sites.
*   **Fifty-Five Villages**: Demolition teams have targeted residential blocks and public infrastructure in 55 border towns.
*   **Civilian Impact**: The destruction of medical clinics and schools has prevented displaced residents from returning.

---

## ## The Jesus Statue Incident: Demolitions in Debel

A specific incident in a Christian border village has caused political friction:
*   **The Incident**: Israeli soldiers destroyed a statue of Jesus Christ during demolition operations in the town of Debel.
*   **Local Anger**: The act drew condemnation from Lebanese Christian communities, who support the ceasefire.
*   **The Compensation**: Rather than disciplining the soldiers, the IDF replaced the statue, which did not resolve local concerns.

---

## ## Retaliatory Fire: Hezbollah Targets Shtoula Gathering

Hezbollah has responded to the demolitions with targeted strikes on border positions:
*   **The Strike**: Hezbollah launched five rockets at an IDF gathering in the settlement of Shtoula.
*   **The Casualties**: The strike wounded at least one Israeli soldier, according to military statements.
*   **Defensive Message**: Faction leaders stated the attack was a response to demolitions in Debel and other border towns.

---

## ## The White House Channel: Bypassing the State Department

Trump has shifted the negotiation process to bypass standard diplomatic channels:
*   **Direct Channel**: Negotiations have been moved directly to the White House, bypassing the State Department.
*   **Netanyahu's Role**: The direct channel allows Trump to coordinate directly with the Israeli Prime Minister.
*   **The Objective**: Establishing a framework that matches U.S. regional goals without intermediate diplomatic layers.

---

## ## Refusing Submission: Lebanon Rejects Ceasefire Under Threat "

The Lebanese government and resistance factions have rejected terms that limit sovereignty:
*   **No Negotiations Under Threat**: Beirut has stated it will not accept a ceasefire that permits unilateral Israeli strikes.
*   **Unified Stance**: Speaker Nabih Berri has coordinated the Lebanese position, rejecting the "peace through strength" model.
*   **Sovereign Control**: Lebanon insists that any security arrangements must be managed by the Lebanese Armed Forces.

---

## ## Intelligence Collection: Trump's Assessment Window

U.S. agencies are using the ceasefire to gather information on the regional leadership:
*   **Assessment Window**: The three-week extension provides time to collect data on the new Iranian leadership.
*   **Target Mapping**: Intelligence services are mapping communication links between Beirut, Damascus, and Tehran.
*   **Evaluating the Blockade**: The window allows the U.S. Navy to measure the economic impact of shipping restrictions.

---

## ## Litani Exclusion Gaps: The Failure to Disarm the Border

The proposed buffer zone remains contested by local forces:
*   **Disarmament Gaps**: The IDF has failed to secure a complete withdrawal of Hezbollah assets from the Litani River sector.
*   **Underground Systems**: Faction units continue to maintain access to tunnel systems near the border.
*   **Failure of Exclusion**: The inability to disarm the border area has led Israeli ministers to demand a resumption of operations.

---

## ## Conclusion: The Fragility of the Three-Week Extension

The proposed three-week ceasefire extension shows the limits of unilateral security arrangements.

While Trump seeks a timeout to gather intelligence and coordinate with Netanyahu, IDF demolitions in Debel and the retaliatory rocket strike in Shtoula show that the truce remains fragile. Hezbollah's response confirms that it will not accept a ceasefire that permits unilateral demolitions. As Lebanon rejects negotiations under threat of air strikes, the three-week window is a temporary pause rather than a path to a stable resolution.
