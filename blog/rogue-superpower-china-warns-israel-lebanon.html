Rogue Superpower: China Warns Israel

July 30, 2026 · 5 min read
Khalid Naami
Founder, Owner, and CEO at Dashboard Options

The Atlantic magazine has labeled the United States a "Rogue Superpower" following Washington's conduct in the Iran war. Robert Kagan wrote that the U.S.-Israeli campaign has strengthened Russia and China, accelerated global chaos, and left America weaker and more isolated than at any point since the 1930s. Meanwhile, China has issued an unprecedented warning to Israel: Lebanon must not become another Gaza. Beijing's intervention at the UN Security Council, combined with economic measures against Israeli shipping, signals the emergence of a new global alignment against American unilateralism.

The Atlantic Verdict: A Rogue Superpower​

The Atlantic's analysis represents the most devastating critique of American foreign policy from within the U.S. establishment:

  • The Label: The magazine branded the United States a "Rogue Superpower" after its unilateral campaign alongside Israel against Iran.
  • Kagan's Warning: Robert Kagan argued that the war has deepened the chasm between the U.S. and its former allies and friends.
  • The Paradox: Even success against Iran would be hollow if it accelerates the collapse of the alliance system that was the real source of American power for eight decades.

The Russian Windfall: Oil Funding Putin's War​

The Iran war has produced unintended strategic consequences that directly benefit Moscow:

  • Oil Price Surge: Before Trump even lifted Russian oil sanctions, prices had skyrocketed, filling Putin's war chest with billions of dollars.
  • Ukraine Dividend: The financial windfall has given Putin more time and more capacity to continue destroying Ukraine's economic infrastructure and energy grid.
  • Interceptor Competition: The U.S. and Ukraine now depend on the same limited supplies of interceptor missiles—every missile diverted to the Gulf leaves Ukrainian cities more vulnerable to Russian strikes.

China's UN Warning: No Second Gaza​

At the UN Security Council on March 31, 2026, China delivered an unprecedented diplomatic intervention:

  • The Demand: Immediate ceasefire and de-escalation in the region.
  • The Warning: Israel must withdraw all forces from Lebanon and respect Lebanese sovereignty, security, and territorial integrity.
  • The Red Line: "Lebanon must not become another Gaza. The international community must not allow the tragedy of Gaza to be repeated."
  • Coalition Building: China is working to form an alliance with Russia and Arab states demanding an immediate ceasefire and stricter conditions for Israeli withdrawal.

The Chinese Threat: Israel's New Front​

For the first time in its history, Israel has acknowledged a direct Chinese threat:

  • The Declaration: Israeli officials stated publicly that Israel is now under a Chinese threat—an unprecedented admission.
  • Economic Measures: China had previously banned COSCO container ships from docking at Israeli ports, signaling further potential economic damage.
  • The Logic: Beijing considers Israel an instrument of a rogue superpower, and therefore an instrument that must be constrained. Every tool of a rogue power is itself rogue.

The Target Bank: CENTCOM's Exhausted Agenda​

The U.S. military's operational objectives in Iran are approaching completion—but not victory:

  • 3,000 of 12,000: CENTCOM has struck 3,000 targets from its original bank of 12,000, focusing on dual-use infrastructure.
  • The Last Target: According to Yedioth Ahronoth, an Israeli official confirmed that the agenda is exhausted—the last target struck was a helicopter repair factory.
  • The Question: With the target bank depleted, how does America manage the post-war phase when Iran retains its strategic missile capability north of the rubble?

Gulf Drain: Allies Funding Their Own Exhaustion​

The financial burden of the war is consuming the Gulf states' reserves:

  • Direct Funding: Gulf monarchies are providing direct financial support for the U.S.-Israeli military campaign.
  • Energy Disruption: The war has paralyzed maritime traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, disrupting the very oil exports that fund Gulf economies.
  • The Trap: By financing a war that threatens their own energy infrastructure, Gulf states are accelerating their own strategic vulnerability.

Israel's Diplomatic Fragmentation Strategy​

Faced with mounting international pressure, Israel has adopted a strategy of separating diplomatic fronts:

  • The Tactic: Israel insists that Hezbollah cannot be linked to the Palestinian cause, and that Iran must be separated from Lebanon in any diplomatic solution.
  • The Objective: Preventing a comprehensive settlement that would address all fronts simultaneously.
  • The Coordination: Israeli officials have called for intensified coordination with the Trump administration to counter growing Chinese diplomatic pressure.

The Gaza Template: Blueprint for Destruction​

China's comparison of Lebanon to Gaza has exposed the underlying pattern of Israeli military operations:

  • The Pattern: Gaza remains the central reference point for every aspect of this conflict—from Operation Al-Aqsa Flood to the war with Iran.
  • The Method: Israel has no strategy beyond the "Gaza model"—destroying Iran by the same methods, and attempting to turn South Lebanon into another Gaza.
  • The Consequence: This unbridled use of force—what China calls "rogue tools of a rogue superpower"—has united the international community against Washington and Tel Aviv.

Conclusion: The Hollow Victory​

The Atlantic's designation of America as a rogue superpower and China's unprecedented intervention at the UN Security Council confirm that the global order has fundamentally shifted.

Even if the military campaign against Iran achieves its tactical objectives, the strategic cost has been catastrophic. The alliance system that sustained American power for eight decades is disintegrating, Russia is being funded by the war's oil price surge, and China has positioned itself as the defender of international law. The U.S. military has exhausted its target bank in Iran while failing to neutralize the strategic missile threat, leaving America isolated, overextended, and facing a multipolar world it can no longer dominate.
