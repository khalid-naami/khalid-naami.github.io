# Iran Nuclear Enrichment: 90% Weapons Threshold

The Iranian Parliament's National Security and Foreign Policy Committee has warned that Tehran will immediately increase its uranium enrichment to 90% if U.S. or Israeli forces initiate a third round of military strikes. The statement, delivered by committee spokesperson Ebrahim Rezaei, marks a change in Iran's nuclear doctrine. The proposal indicates that a third conflict would trigger the implementation of a national defense mandate, bypassing historical fatwas to secure strategic deterrence.

## Strategic Warning: Ebrahim Rezaei Reasserts the Ninety-Percent Option

The Iranian Parliament has formally introduced the option to escalate enrichment in response to new attacks:
*   **The Statement**: Ebrahim Rezaei warned that a new attack would lead to immediate enrichment to 90%.
*   **Parliamentary Mandate**: The committee has prepared a draft bill to authorize the escalation if military facilities are targeted.
*   **The Intent**: The move is intended to establish a clear threshold to deter further air strikes.

---

## The Jurisprudential Doctrine: Bypassing the Historical Fatwa

The political leadership has indicated that the concept of national survival overrides previous policy limits:
*   **Jurisprudential Review**: In the event of a threat to national survival, previous restrictions on enrichment can be suspended.
*   **Bypassing the Fatwa**: The new leadership argues that the historical fatwa against weapons development does not apply during active war.
*   **The Doctrine**: The concept of "defense of the state" is presented as the primary legal and religious requirement.

---

## National Defense Mandate: Mojtaba Khamenei's Command Stance

The decision-making process has been centralized under the new leadership in Tehran:
*   **Mojtaba Khamenei**: The leader has coordinated directly with military commanders to define the thresholds for escalation.
*   **Centralized Authority**: Khamenei's stance has unified hardline and reformist factions around the defense strategy.
*   **No Compromise**: The leadership has rejected U.S. requests to negotiate limits on enrichment or missile programs.

---

## Deep Under-Surface Storage: Bypassing the GBU-57 Bunker Buster

Iran's critical nuclear assets are stored in heavily fortified facilities:
*   **Fordow Bunkers**: Key centrifuges and enriched material are located in deep underground facilities at Fordow.
*   **GBU-57 Immunity**: The facilities are situated at depths that protect them from U.S. GBU-57 bunker-buster bombs.
*   **Fortified Security**: The underground design ensures that the material cannot be destroyed by conventional air strikes.

---

## The US Air Force Dilemma: No Actionable Plan for Fuel Removal

U.S. agencies have acknowledged the limitations of military options for neutralizing the enrichment program:
*   **US Air Force Assessment**: The Pentagon has confirmed it lacks a viable plan to physically remove or destroy the buried material.
*   **No Access**: The depth of the bunkers prevents ground teams from accessing the material without a full-scale occupation.
*   **Technical Limit**: Air strikes would only seal the entrances, leaving the interior infrastructure intact.

## Threshold Calculations: The Four Hundred Forty Kilogram Inventory

The size of Iran's enriched material inventory has been audited by international monitors:
*   **The Inventory**: The IAEA estimated that Iran possesses approximately 440 kilograms of 60% enriched uranium.
*   **Weapons Capacity**: According to technical assessments, this inventory is sufficient to produce 10 nuclear devices if enriched to 90%.
*   **Conversion Time**: Analysts estimate the conversion of this material to weapons-grade would require less than two weeks.

---

## Sub-Surface Retaliation: The USS Alaska Deployment in Gibraltar

The U.S. has deployed strategic assets to show its commitment to regional deterrence:
*   **USS Alaska**: The Ohio-class nuclear submarine *USS Alaska* was deployed to the Mediterranean, transiting Gibraltar.
*   **Trident Missiles**: The submarine carries 20 Trident II D5 ballistic missiles.
*   **Show of Force**: The deployment is intended as a warning against any move to cross the 90% enrichment threshold.

---

## Deterrence Parity: Dimona as the Primary Iranian Target

Iran has identified key installations that would be targeted in a retaliatory strike:
*   **Dimona Target**: Iranian commanders stated that any strike on Fordow would result in a retaliatory strike on the Dimona nuclear facility.
*   **Target Selection**: The selection of Dimona is intended to establish parity in target options.
*   **Strategic Deterrence**: The threat of mutual nuclear facility strikes is designed to prevent escalation by both sides.

---

## Conclusion: The Final Jurisprudential Defense Doctrine of Tehran

The warning from the Iranian Parliament regarding 90% uranium enrichment shows the change in Iran's nuclear strategy.

By preparing a draft bill to escalate enrichment in the event of a third round of strikes, Tehran has established a clear threshold. The deep under-surface storage at Fordow, combined with the 440 kg inventory of 60% enriched uranium, provides a technical basis for this strategy. As the U.S. deploys the *USS Alaska* to Gibraltar, the threat of mutual strikes on Dimona and Fordow confirms that the nuclear standoff has entered a new phase of strategic deterrence.
