# IDF 98th Division Withdraws from South Lebanon

The Israeli Defense Forces have executed a sudden withdrawal of the elite 98th Division from southern Lebanon. The decision, backed by senior military observers, follows a rise in personnel losses in forward combat sectors. The withdrawal highlights the challenge of maintaining ground forces in areas exposed to advanced anti-tank guided missiles and fiber-optic guided drones, which bypass standard electronic countermeasures.

## Shock Withdrawal: The 98th Division Leaves Southern Lebanon

The elite paratrooper and commando division has completed its redeployment back across the border:
*   **The Command**: General Staff officers ordered the 98th Division to withdraw from forward areas in southern Lebanon.
*   **Rapid Evacuation**: The movement was executed during the night to minimize exposure to retaliatory fire.
*   **Strategic Shift**: The withdrawal of the elite division represents a change in Israel's operational goals in the northern theatre.

---

## Gabi Ashkenazi's Warning: The Existential Threat to Ground Forces

Senior military analysts have supported the decision to reduce troop exposure:
*   **Ashkenazi's Warning**: Former Chief of Staff Gabi Ashkenazi stated that maintaining forward positions posed an existential risk to ground forces.
*   **No Clear Mission**: Ashkenazi argued that the campaign lacked a defined political objective, exposing soldiers to unnecessary risks.
*   **Refusing Commitments**: The military leadership refused to commit additional reserves to sustain the offensive.

---

## Unsustainable Losses: One Thousand Casualties in Forward Sectors

The rate of casualties among Israeli ground forces influenced the decision:
*   **The Metrics**: Cumulative casualties across the divisions deployed in the north reached approximately 1,000 soldiers.
*   **Force Preservation**: The scale of losses threatened the operational capacity of elite brigade structures.
*   **Public Concern**: Rising casualty figures have increased public concern regarding the management of the campaign.

---

## The Failure of "Arrows of the North": Unhandled Radwan Tunnels

The operations failed to dismantle the underground systems built by the adversary:
*   **The Claim**: Official statements asserted that the operation had cleared the border zone of infiltration threats.
*   **The Reality**: Underground assembly points remained functional, allowing defensive operations to continue.
*   **Radwan Strength**: Elite Radwan units retained the capacity to execute counter-attacks from underground positions.

---

## Tunnels in Qantara: The Discovery of Arad and Acre Networks

Two strategic tunnel complexes were identified near the town of al-Qantara:
*   **Arad and Acre**: Large tunnel systems, named Arad and Acre, were discovered close to the border.
*   **Fortified Positions**: The complexes contained communications equipment, weapons depots, and living quarters.
*   **No Penetration**: The failure to detect or neutralize these complexes before the advance exposed the vulnerability of the troop movements.

---

## Fiber-Optic Guided Drones: Hezbollah's Jamming-Resistant Arsenal

The deployment of new drone technology has affected the performance of air defense networks:
*   **Fiber-Optic Guidance**: Adversary forces deployed drones guided by physical fiber-optic cables.
*   **No Jamming**: The physical connection makes the guidance systems immune to electronic warfare and radio frequency jamming.
*   **Targeting Armor**: The drones have been used to execute precision strikes on armored vehicles and command posts.

---

## Defensive Re-alignments: Leaving the 36th Division Isolated

The departure of the commandos has left remaining forces in a defensive posture:
*   **36th Division**: The 36th Division is now the sole major force executing maneuvers in the area.
*   **Support Roles**: The 91st (Galilee) and 146th (Reserve) Divisions have shifted to defensive positions to protect the border.
*   **Reduced Scope**: The remaining units are focused on holding existing positions rather than advancing further.

---

## Netanyahu's Admission: A Stalled Strategy and Calls to Trump

The political leadership has acknowledged the limitations of the current campaign:
*   **The Admission**: Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu admitted to his cabinet that military options in the north have run their course.
*   **Dahiya Limit**: Netanyahu stated that the destruction of infrastructure had not halted the retaliatory strikes.
*   **Trump Coordination**: Netanyahu contacted U.S. President Donald Trump to coordinate on economic and shipping restrictions.

---

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Limit of Israeli Ground Maneuvers

The withdrawal of the IDF 98th Division from southern Lebanon shows the limits of the ground campaign.

By withdrawing the commandos to reduce casualties, Israel has acknowledged the challenges of managing fiber-optic drones and fortified tunnel systems like Arad and Acre. With the 36th Division isolated and Gabi Ashkenazi warning of existential risks, the retreat confirms that airstrikes have not resolved the border threat. As Netanyahu coordinates with Trump on shipping blockades, the military shift shows a pivot away from costly ground operations.
