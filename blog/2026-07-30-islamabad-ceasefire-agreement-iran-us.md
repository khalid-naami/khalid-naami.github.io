# Islamabad Ceasefire: 10-Point Plan and Mossad Defeat

A 15-day temporary ceasefire has been established in the Middle East following intensive backchannel negotiations brokered by Pakistan in Islamabad. Led by U.S. Vice President JD Vance, the agreement utilizes an Iranian-drafted 10-Point Plan as the framework for negotiations, completely bypassing Israeli demands. Concurrently, a major leak has exposed the cancellation of a joint CIA-Mossad secret plan to overthrow the Iranian regime, triggering a political crisis in Tel Aviv where opposition leaders are calling the truce a strategic defeat.

## The Islamabad Channel: Vance Brokers 15-Day Truce

The diplomatic breakthrough was achieved through a dedicated backchannel in Pakistan:
*   **The Mediator**: Pakistan's military intelligence hosted the negotiations in Islamabad, coordinating directly with U.S. and Iranian delegations.
*   **Vance's Leadership**: Vice President JD Vance led the U.S. side, having consistently opposed the expansion of the Gulf conflict due to energy security concerns.
*   **The Truce**: A 15-day halt in direct military strikes was agreed upon to finalize the terms of a permanent settlement.

## The 10-Point Plan: Iran Dictates Ceasefire Terms

The negotiation draft is based on a 10-Point Plan submitted by Iran's Supreme National Security Council:
*   **Hormuz Sovereignty**: Acceptance of Iran's right to establish transit protocols and collect shipping fees in the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **Sanctions Relief**: The lifting of all primary and secondary U.S. sanctions, alongside IAEA and UNSC negative resolutions.
*   **War Compensation**: Payment of financial damages to Iran for strikes executed during the campaign.
*   **Axis Protections**: A complete halt to hostile operations against the axis of resistance in Lebanon, Iraq, Yemen, and Palestine.

Netanyahu's administration was completely excluded from the diplomatic channel:
*   **No Israeli Presence**: The Pakistani hosts, representing a nuclear-armed state with no diplomatic links to Tel Aviv, refused to permit any Israeli delegation to attend the venue.
*   **Neutral Phrasing**: The draft agreement omits any mention of Israel, referencing only "the United States and its allies, and Iran and its allies."
*   **Netanyahu's Frustration**: Israeli Defense Minister Katz threatened to sabotage the agreement if Israel's security requirements were not explicitly codified.

## The Oil Clause: Tehran Closes Hormuz to Crude Tankers

Tehran has used its maritime control to pressure the negotiations:
*   **The Blockade Adjust**: In response to Israel's attempts to exclude Lebanon from the ceasefire, the IRGC modified its Hormuz transit protocol.
*   **Oil Exclusion**: While non-oil commercial shipping is permitted to pass under Iranian clearance, all crude oil tankers remain banned from transit.
*   **Energy Pressure**: The exclusion maintains high global oil prices, forcing Washington to pressure Tel Aviv to respect the unified axis ceasefire.
