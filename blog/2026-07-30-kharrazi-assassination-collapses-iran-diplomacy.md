# Kharrazi Targeted: Iran Diplomacy Collapses

Israel has targeted Kamal Kharrazi—Iran's former Foreign Minister and one of the most influential figures in Tehran's foreign policy establishment—in a strike on a residential building in the Iranian capital. According to the New York Times, Kharrazi was severely wounded and his wife was killed. Tehran believes the attack was designed to sabotage a potential communication channel with Washington, as Kharrazi had been identified by the CIA as a key interlocutor for a meeting with Vice President JD Vance. His refusal to attend that meeting became his death warrant.
