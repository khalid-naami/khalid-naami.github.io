# Russia Target Map: CEPS Pipeline & Greek Base

Russia has published a second target map detailing strategic military infrastructure across the European Union. The map identifies the Central Europe Pipeline System (CEPS), an underground network of over 5,400 km supplying fuel to major U.S. and NATO airbases. In Greece, the map highlights the vulnerability of the newly established U.S. logistics hub at Alexandroupolis, which lacks Patriot or THAAD air defense coverage.

## Strategic Target Mapping: Moscow's Second European Logistics Audit

Russian defense authorities have updated their catalog of potential strike targets in Europe:
*   **The Target Map**: A detailed map of critical logistics nodes across Western Europe was published.
*   **Logistics Focus**: The audit prioritizes fuel transit networks, ports, and transit rails.
*   **Deterrence Role**: The publication warns Western states against allowing deep-range strikes on Russian territory.

---

## The CEPS Infrastructure: Central Europe's Underground Fuel Network

The map details the layout of the primary military fuel pipeline network:
*   **The CEPS**: The Central Europe Pipeline System spans 5,484 km under the ground.
*   **Coverage**: The pipeline runs through France, Germany, Belgium, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands.
*   **Capacity**: The system replaces approximately 1,200 tank trucks per day in operational transport.

---

## Ramstein and NATO Bases: Disruption Vectors for Aviation Fuel

The pipeline network is critical for maintaining aviation operations in the European theater:
*   **Ramstein Air Base**: The main U.S. air transport hub in Germany receives its fuel directly from CEPS.
*   **Disruption Impact**: Severing the network would halt continuous combat flights within hours.
*   **Vulnerable Pump Stations**: The map identifies key pumping and distribution centers as primary targets.

## The Alexandroupolis Exposure: Greek Port Lacks Air Defense Screens

The Greek port of Alexandroupolis has been highlighted as a major defensive vulnerability:
*   **Alexandroupolis Hub**: The port serves as the primary entry point for U.S. heavy armor in the Balkans.
*   **No Air Defense**: The base lacks dedicated Patriot or THAAD air defense screens.
*   **Target Status**: The lack of defensive cover makes the base vulnerable to long-range missile strikes.

---

## Rail Logistics to the East: Heavy Tank Transit in Twenty-Four Hours

The port's strategic value lies in its rail connections to Eastern Europe:
*   **Rail Transit**: Heavy tanks and artillery are transported directly from the port to Romania and Bulgaria.
*   **Speed Advantage**: The rail network allows delivery of combat equipment within 24 hours.
*   **Bypassing Straits**: The land route allows U.S. forces to bypass naval restrictions in the Bosporus.
