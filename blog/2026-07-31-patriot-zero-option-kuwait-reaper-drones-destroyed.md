# Patriot Zero Option: Kuwait Reaper Defeat

The air defense situation in Kuwait has degraded to the "Patriot Zero Option" following drone and missile strikes. In the last 24 hours, three Patriot launchers and a radar array were hit, bringing the total number of destroyed batteries to 20. The strikes also targeted flight lines, destroying five U.S. MQ-9 Reaper drones, as U.S. planners face a strategic stalemate in the Gulf.
