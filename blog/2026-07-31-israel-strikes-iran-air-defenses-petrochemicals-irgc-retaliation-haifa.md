# Israel Strikes Iran Petrochemicals: Haifa Hit

Israel has executed a coordinated air campaign targeting Iranian air defense systems and petrochemical infrastructure. The strikes, which involved ground-to-ground ballistic missiles, were met with immediate IRGC retaliatory salvos targeting Nevatim Airbase, Tel Nof Airbase, and a petrochemical refinery in Haifa. The targeting of energy infrastructure has triggered a panic on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange and caused global oil prices to rise.

## Operation Victory Counterpart: Israel's Coordinated Salvos on Iran

Israeli forces have executed strikes targeting military and industrial sectors in Iran:
*   **The Campaign**: The strikes were directed by Chief of Staff Eyal Zamir from a command bunker.
*   **Ballistic Missiles**: Israel utilized 20 ground-to-ground ballistic missiles for the first time.
*   **Aviation Role**: IAF wings supported the operation using standoff weapons.

---

## Defense Suppression: Suppressing Nine Iranian Air Shield batteries

The primary military objective of the initial wave was disabling defensive detection screens:
*   **Nine Systems**: Israeli strikes targeted nine Iranian air defense battery locations.
*   **Radar Disruption**: The attacks aimed to degrade tracking radars along transit corridors.
*   **Radar Reduction**: Iranian reports estimate a temporary 42% reduction in regional detection coverage.

---

## Petrochemical Targets: Strikes on Hamadan and Central Energy Sites

The strikes targeted industrial energy infrastructure to create economic pressure:
*   **Petrochemical Sites**: Three petrochemical installations, including one in Hamadan, were struck.
*   **Civilians Sites**: The target bank included locations near Tehran University Space Center.
*   **Basij Bases**: Auxiliary security installations in Hamadan were included in the strike path.

---

## US-CENTCOM Coordination: The Secret Calls of Zamir and Kurilla

The operation was executed with direct coordination and intelligence support from Washington:
*   **Zamir and Kurilla**: Chief of Staff Zamir held coordination calls with CENTCOM's General Michael Kurilla.
*   **Target Review**: The target bank was submitted to U.S. defense officials before execution.
*   **Official Denial**: U.S. spokespersons denied participation to avoid direct involvement in the conflict.

---

## The Iranian Retaliation: Hypersonic Salvos and New Drone Classes

The IRGC responded by launching high-velocity salvos and deploying new weapons classes:
*   **High-Velocity Salvos**: Precision ballistic missiles targeted Nevatim and Tel Nof airbases.
*   **New Drones**: The IRGC deployed unprecedented attack drone models during the retaliation.
*   **Command Control**: General Hossein Mohebi declared that airspace over the targets remains contested.

---

## The Haifa Petrochemical Hit: Direct Strike on Israel's Energy Center

A retaliatory strike targeted energy processing installations in northern Israel:
*   **Haifa Strike**: A petrochemical installation in Haifa was hit by an IRGC cruise missile.
*   **Refinery Damage**: Storage tanks and processing components sustained significant damage.
*   **Deterrent Action**: The hit is intended to establish parity with strikes on Iranian energy sites.

---

## Stock Market Panic: Tel Aviv Exchange Indices Collapse

The targeting of energy infrastructure had immediate consequences for financial markets:
*   **Index Drop**: The Tel Aviv Stock Exchange energy index fell by 2.5% to 5%.
*   **Banking Losses**: Financial and banking shares recorded losses of approximately 1%.
*   **Dollar Spike**: The U.S. dollar spiked against the Shekel, approaching a rate of 3.7.
*   **Oil Prices**: Global crude oil prices rose by $4 per barrel following the strikes.
