Robert Kagan wrote that the U.S.-Israeli campaign has strengthened Russia and China, accelerated global chaos, and left America weaker and more isolated than at any point since the 1930s. Beijing's intervention at the UN Security Council, combined with economic measures against Israeli shipping, signals the emergence of a new global alignment against American unilateralism.
