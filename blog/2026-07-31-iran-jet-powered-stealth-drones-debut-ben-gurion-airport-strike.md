# Iran Jet-Powered Drones Debut: Ben Gurion Hit

Iran has deployed its newly developed jet-powered suicide drones, including the Shahdana, during the recent 12-hour air battle. Breaking away from slow piston-engine Shahed-136 models, the high-speed stealth drones struck multiple targets, including the fuel depot at Ben Gurion Airport. Israel responded by launching Rampage and Anchor standoff cruise missiles targeting drone facilities in Hamadan, amid confusion over the destruction of a mysterious air defense system.

## Unmanned Transition: Piston Engines Replaced by Jet Propulsion

The transition to jet-powered propulsion marks a shift in Iran's drone development:
*   **The Transition**: Piston engines have been replaced by micro-turbojet units in new classes.
*   **Speed Advantage**: Flight speeds have increased from 180 km/h to over 500 km/h.
*   **Response Window**: The high speed reduces the reaction window for defense batteries.

---

## True Promise Four: The Debut of the Jet-Powered Shahdana

The new platforms were utilized during the retaliatory strikes named Operation True Promise IV:
*   **Shahdana Debut**: The jet-powered Shahdana suicide drone was deployed for the first time.
*   **Simultaneous Launch**: The drones were launched alongside Kheibar Shekan ballistic missiles.
*   **Target Metrics**: The high-speed salvos successfully targeted 16 designated locations.

---

## Targeting Ben Gurion: Precision Hits on Airport Fuel Depots

One of the primary targets of the jet-propelled drones was Ben Gurion Airport's logistics sector:
*   **The Strike**: Drones executed a precision strike on the airport's central fuel depot.
*   **Explosion**: The impact triggered a major fire, requiring emergency response teams.
*   **Chemical Move**: The strike occurred after authorities had moved sensitive chemical stores out.

---

## Grounding the Tankers: Disrupting Long-Range Flight Operations

The strike on the airport's fuel depot directly affected military aviation operations:
*   **Tanker Fuels**: The destroyed depot contained fuel designated for aerial refueling tankers.
*   **Tankers Grounded**: The lack of fuel grounded refueling tankers used for long-range missions.
*   **Tactical Limit**: The disruption restricted the range of Israeli combat squadrons.

---

## The Israeli Response: Standoff Strikes with Rampage and Anchor

Israeli forces executed immediate retaliatory strikes using standoff cruise missiles:
*   **Rampage Missiles**: Supersonic Rampage missiles were launched from standoff platforms.
*   **Anchor Rockets**: Anchor target/combat rockets were used to penetrate air shields.
*   **Standoff Launch**: The launches were executed from airspace outside Iranian territorial borders.

---

## Targeting Hamadan: Attacks on UAV Assembly and Command Facilities

The Israeli response targeted the assembly facilities of the new jet drones:
*   **Hamadan Strike**: Standoff missiles targeted assembly workshops in Hamadan.
*   **Basij Posts**: Auxiliary support structures used by Basij security units were hit.
*   **Command Loss**: The strikes aimed to degrade command coordination for drone wings.

---

## The Launcher Confusion: Mystery Surrounding Air Shield Explosions

The strikes on Hamadan resulted in secondary explosions, creating debate among experts:
*   **Launcher Destroyed**: A mysterious air defense launcher was destroyed in the strike.
*   **Unknown Model**: Defense experts were unable to identify the launcher's origin or type.
*   **Decoy Theory**: Some analysts suggest the hit target was a decoy containing fuel cells.

---

## Technical Mechanics: Bypassing Regional Radar Detection Screens

The Shahdana drone utilizes specific features to bypass regional detection screens:
*   **Low Silhouette**: The compact airframe design minimizes radar cross-section.
*   **Thermal Control**: The jet exhaust is mixed with ambient air to limit infrared signatures.
*   **Flight Path**: The drone utilizes terrain-following flight paths to bypass search radars.

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Shift in Night Precision Strikes

The debut of Iran's jet-powered suicide drones and the strike on Ben Gurion Airport show the shift in asymmetric precision strike capabilities.

By replacing piston engines with jet propulsion, developers have increased flight speeds and limited the effectiveness of defense batteries. The grounding of aerial refueling tankers at Ben Gurion and the subsequent standoff missile strikes in Hamadan show the tactical consequences of the standoff. As experts analyze the destroyed air defense components, these developments confirm that high-speed stealth drones present new challenges to conventional air defense networks.
