# Stoning Trump Effigy: F-15 Tech Exposed

During the funeral of Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, public anger was demonstrated through a symbolic "stoning of Trump" effigy ritual in Tehran. The events coincided with Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz officially admitting Tel Aviv's responsibility for Khamenei's assassination. Additionally, regional intelligence reports indicate that shot-down U.S. and Israeli F-15 fighter jets have allowed Iranian and Turkish engineers to reverse-engineer key electronics and radar systems.

## Stoning of Trump: The Symbolic Effigy Ritual in Tehran

Public anger over the assassination of Ayatollah Khamenei was demonstrated through symbolic rituals:
*   **The Ritual**: Mourners in Tehran participated in the stoning of an effigy representing Donald Trump.
*   **Hajj Imagery**: The ritual was modeled after the stoning of Satan during the Hajj pilgrimage.
*   **Political Message**: The action demonstrated popular rejection of U.S. involvement in the strike.

Following the appearance of Khamenei's sons, the Israeli Defense Minister officially admitted responsibility:

*   **Lineage Failure**: The admission aimed to counter reports of the failure of the "lineage termination" promise.
*   **Diplomatic Split**: The confession split Tel Aviv from U.S. efforts to portray the strike as independent.
