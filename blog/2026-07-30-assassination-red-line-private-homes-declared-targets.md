# Assassination Red Line: Private Homes Declared Targets

The conflict between the U.S.-led coalition and Iran has entered a dangerous phase of personal targeting. The IRGC has issued a warning declaring the private residences of senior U.S. and Israeli military and political officials in the Middle East as legitimate military targets. This shift, announced by the Joint Military Command spokesman Ibrahim Zolfagari, comes alongside a major structural change in Tehran: the appointment of hardline IRGC veteran Mohammad Baqer Zolqadr as the new Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, replacing the assassinated Ali Larijani.

## The Symmetrical Threat: Declaring Private Homes as Targets

The warning issued by Joint Military Command spokesperson Ibrahim Zolfagari represents a shift in targeting methodology:
*   **The Targets**: Private residences of U.S. and Israeli military officers, diplomats, and political figures stationed or residing in the Middle East.
*   **The Scope**: The threat covers residences in regional host nations and within Israel itself.
*   **The Justification**: Zolfagari stated that the decision was a direct response to U.S. and Israeli airstrikes targeting the private residences of Iranian scientists, commanders, and officials.

---

## The Assassination of Ali Larijani: A Strategic Blunder

The IRGC's shift to personal targeting was triggered by the assassination of Ali Larijani, the former SNSC Secretary. Larijani was killed on March 17 in a targeted strike on his daughter's home, alongside his son Morteza.

By targeting Larijani, U.S. and Israeli commands eliminated the last major moderate figure in Tehran who possessed the authority to negotiate a diplomatic ceasefire. Larijani had served as a key channel for backchannel communications, and his death has closed the path for diplomatic mediation.

---

## The Rise of Mohammad Baqer Zolqadr

Following Larijani's assassination, the Iranian leadership bypassed diplomatic figures and appointed Mohammad Baqer Zolqadr as the new Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC).

Zolqadr is a veteran IRGC general who has spent his entire career in the military-intelligence establishment. Having served as the Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the IRGC between 1997 and 2005 and later as Deputy Interior Minister for Security, Zolqadr is a hardliner with no history of diplomatic engagement with the West. His appointment confirms that the security apparatus has assumed direct control of national policy.

---

## Sidelining the Pezeshkian Reformist Cabinet

Zolqadr's appointment represents the consolidation of the military establishment over the civil government of President Masoud Pezeshkian:
*   **Decoupled policy**: Decisions regarding the nuclear program and regional strategy are now managed directly by Zolqadr and the IRGC command.
*   **No compromises**: The civil cabinet has been sidelined from backchannel discussions, neutralizing the influence of reformist diplomats who favored compromise.
*   **Unified front**: The SNSC's policy is now aligned with the IRGC's doctrine of asymmetric resistance.

---

## The 15-Point Capitulation Draft and the Deadlock

The transition to a military-dominated command in Tehran has been accelerated by the uncompromising terms of the U.S. peace draft.

The 15 points presented by the Trump administration left no room for reciprocal concessions, demanding the immediate surrender of Iran's nuclear material and the dismantling of its missile force. By offering no diplomatic exit, Washington confirmed to the Iranian establishment that the U.S. objective is capitulation, leaving Zolqadr with no option but to prepare the country for a prolonged war.

---

## The Expiration of the Ceasefire Window

The diplomatic deadlock occurs as the temporary pause proposed by the Trump administration is set to expire this Saturday:
*   **Saturday deadline**: The 10-day extension of the pause on energy strikes ends without a meeting.
*   **No negotiations**: Zolqadr has refused to engage in discussions based on the 15-point draft.
*   **Preparation for strikes**: Both sides have utilized the window to prepare for high-intensity operations, with the U.S. deploying 10,000 troops and the IRGC dispersing its missile assets.

---

## The Drone Threat: Bypassing the VIP Shields

The IRGC's capability to execute its personal targeting threat has been demonstrated by the performance of its suicide drones:
*   **The Netanyahu incident**: A low-flying drone successfully reached the private residence of Prime Minister Netanyahu, prompting a reinforcement of his security protocols.
*   **The AWACS lesson**: IRGC commanders argue that if their Shahed-136 drones can bypass Patriot batteries to destroy a strategic E-3 AWACS on the ground, they can easily locate and strike the residential villas of regional decision-makers.

---

## The Regional Target List: Safe Houses Under Watch

Under Zolqadr's direction, IRGC intelligence has begun compiling a database of the private residences used by Western and Israeli officials in the region.

The list includes diplomatic compounds, temporary military quarters, and safe houses in regional capitals. By declaring these civilian sites as legitimate targets, Iran is signaling that U.S. and Israeli officials cannot secure their safety by dispersing into civilian areas, increasing the political risk for host nations.

---

## Conclusion: The Era of Personal Targeting

The appointment of Mohammad Baqer Zolqadr as SNSC chief and the declaration of private residences as military targets confirm that the war has entered a phase of personal confrontation.

By assassinating Ali Larijani, the U.S. and Israel eliminated the diplomatic bridge that could have facilitated a ceasefire. Under Zolqadr's command, the IRGC has assumed control of Iran's national security policy, rejecting the 15-point capitulation draft and preparing for a war of attrition. The threat to target the private homes of Western and Israeli officials proves that the conflict is no longer confined to the battlefield, but has become a personal war where decision-makers on both sides are within target coordinates.
