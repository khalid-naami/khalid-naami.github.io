# Wave 9: Cluster Warheads Reach Israel

Iran's IRGC launched its ninth coordinated missile wave against Israel during the first night of Passover, firing over 100 missiles alongside simultaneous strikes from Lebanon and Yemen. For the first time, ten missiles struck Israeli territory from end to end. The Israeli Broadcasting Authority confirmed a critical detail: while ballistic missiles were intercepted, all cluster warhead missiles reached their targets. In south Tel Aviv, a six-story building collapsed, forcing the evacuation of 100 IDF soldiers disguised as civilian tenants.

## The Ninth Wave: Multi-Front Saturation

The IRGC's ninth wave represented an escalation in both scale and coordination across the resistance axis:
*   **Iran**: At least 14 ballistic missiles were launched directly from Iranian territory.
*   **Lebanon**: Hezbollah fired 150 rockets and drones, targeting northern Israel and the Golan Heights.
*   **Yemen**: Ansar Allah launched long-range strikes toward Eilat and southern military installations.
*   **Total**: Iran confirmed the combined total exceeded 100 missiles across all three fronts.

---

## Cluster Warheads: The Interception Gap

The Israeli Broadcasting Authority inadvertently revealed a critical gap in Israel's air defense coverage:
*   **The Admission**: All ballistic missiles were intercepted. However, all cluster warhead missiles reached their targets.
*   **Channel 2 Contradiction**: A secondary broadcast claimed complete interception, but excluded cluster munitions from its count entirely.
*   **The Implication**: Israel's defensive systems—Arrow, David's Sling, and Iron Dome—have no reliable solution for cluster-type warheads, which disperse submunitions over wide areas before interception is possible.

---

## Building Collapse: 100 Soldiers Evacuated

The most significant ground impact occurred in the Petah Tikva area, south of Tel Aviv:
*   **The Hit**: The two upper floors of a six-story residential building collapsed "like a pancake" (Ma'ariv's description).
*   **The Evacuation**: Mayor Rami Greenberg confirmed at 1:27 that 100 individuals were evacuated from the building and surrounding area.
*   **The Cover**: Magen David Adom publicly reported evacuating a 62-year-old woman and a 35-year-old man to Ichilov Hospital—civilian casualties only.
*   **The Reality**: Two separate official statements revealed the discrepancy: the evacuees were predominantly young IDF reservists housed as civilian tenants by the Ministry of Defense.

---

## The Lava Unit: Concealing Military Casualties

The deployment of Israel's specialized rescue assets revealed the severity of the incident:
*   **Unit Lava**: The national special rescue unit (Lahava), attached to the Fire and Rescue Service, was deployed for complex extraction operations.
*   **Road Closures**: Police sealed all access roads to the impact site—a measure disproportionate for a civilian building collapse.
*   **The Brigade**: Northern Command's Daniel Haddad and additional forces were dispatched for the evacuation, confirming the military nature of the casualties.

---

## Golan Logistics: The Strategic Corridor Under Fire

For the first time, Iran and Hezbollah launched coordinated strikes against the Golan Heights with a specific operational objective:
*   **The Target**: Military logistics routes that pass through the Golan, which serve as the primary supply corridor for IDF operations in the north.
*   **Joint Strikes**: Both Iranian and Lebanese-origin missiles struck Golan positions simultaneously.
*   **Hezbollah Statements**: Official statements confirmed the targeting of logistics infrastructure, signaling an attempt to sever the IDF's supply chain to the Lebanese front.

---

## Amazon Bahrain: Striking the Digital Backbone

The IRGC extended its target selection beyond military assets to the technological infrastructure supporting the coalition:
*   **The Target**: Amazon Web Services' primary cloud data center in Bahrain—the largest in the Middle East.
*   **Financial Times Confirmation**: The strike caused significant damage to the facility's server infrastructure.
*   **Inside Israel**: Additional strikes targeted technology companies operating within Israeli territory, marking a new phase of hybrid warfare.
*   **The Doctrine**: Every assassination of an Iranian figure now triggers retaliatory strikes against two Western technology companies.

---

## Passover Symbolism: The Religious Dimension

The timing of the ninth wave was calculated for maximum psychological impact:
*   **First Night of Passover**: The strikes coincided with the beginning of the Jewish holiday, disrupting celebrations across Israel.
*   **Religious Comparison**: Israel had previously denied Muslims Eid al-Fitr prayers and blocked Christians from Easter observances—the Passover strikes were framed as a response.
*   **Katz's Threat**: Defense Minister Israel Katz threatened Hezbollah's Secretary-General Naim Qassem, promising to send him to "the lowest circle of hell."

---

## Israel's Response: Striking Western Tehran

In response to the Passover strikes, the IDF launched retaliatory attacks on Iranian residential areas:
*   **The Target**: Western Tehran neighborhoods, with the stated objective of deterring further attacks during the holiday.
*   **The Logic**: Striking civilian infrastructure was intended to signal that continued attacks during Passover would carry a disproportionate cost.
*   **The Weather Factor**: Dense cloud cover over Israel had been disrupting IDF air operations, but Iran's forces successfully launched through the adverse weather conditions.

---

## Conclusion: The Cluster Warhead Paradigm Shift

The ninth wave has exposed a structural vulnerability in Israel's multi-layered air defense system.

The confirmed penetration of cluster warheads—acknowledged by Israel's own broadcasting authority—demonstrates that Iran has identified and exploited a gap in the interception architecture. The evacuation of 100 IDF soldiers from a collapsed building in Petah Tikva, disguised as civilian tenants, reveals the extent to which the military has embedded itself within civilian infrastructure. Combined with the strategic targeting of Golan logistics corridors and Western technology centers, the ninth wave marks a turning point in the conflict's trajectory.
