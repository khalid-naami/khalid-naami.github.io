# 332nd Wing Strike: Jordan Base Burnout

Iranian precision missile strikes on Jordan's Muwaffaq Salti Air Base have degraded the U.S. 332nd Air Expeditionary Wing's operations. The strikes reduced the airbase's operational capacity to 42%, forcing CENTCOM to shift command centers to local hotels, which were subsequently targeted. The systematic burnout of Jordan's bases has compromised the forward air buffer that protects Israel's eastern front.

## 332nd Air Expeditionary Wing: The Strike on the Levant Command Node

The Iranian missile campaign targeted the primary U.S. tactical air command in Jordan:
*   **The Target**: The command structures of the U.S. 332nd Air Expeditionary Wing.
*   **Location**: Muwaffaq Salti Air Base (Al-Azraq) in northern Jordan.
*   **Tactical Focus**: The strikes focused on the command, communications, and intelligence nodes.

## Operational Capacity Cut: Reducing Al-Azraq to Forty-Two Percent

The damage to base infrastructure has restricted flight operations:
*   **Capacity Reduction**: The airbase's operational capacity fell to 42% following the strikes.
*   **Runway Damage**: Multiple missile hits damaged the runways, limiting takeoff intervals.
*   **Logistics Backlog**: Ground support systems suffered damage, delaying fighter maintenance.

## Hotel Command Relocation: CENTCOM Moves to Temporary Nodes

The destruction of primary command blocks forced U.S. officers to relocate operations:
*   **Temporary Nodes**: Command functions were moved to hotels near the airbase.
*   **Operational Stance**: Planners utilized emergency mobile links to maintain contact with aircraft.
*   **Subsequent Hits**: Drone strikes targeted the temporary hotel locations, disrupting the links.

## Systematic Base Burnout: Targeting the Three Jordanian Outposts

The campaign in Jordan is designed to systematically disable U.S. staging assets:
*   **Three Outposts**: Strikes targeted Muwaffaq Salti, H-5, and King Faisal airbases.
*   **Burnout Strategy**: Targeting multiple bases prevents the relocation of assets within Jordan.
*   **Expeditionary Limits**: The campaign has neutralized the primary staging grounds for U.S. Levant operations.

The degradation of Jordan's air defense assets directly affects Israeli security planning:

*   **Eastern Border Exposure**: The loss of the shield leaves Israel's eastern border exposed to direct salvos.
