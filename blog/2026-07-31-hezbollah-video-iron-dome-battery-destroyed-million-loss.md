# Hezbollah Video: Iron Dome Battery Destroyed

A 1-minute 39-second video published by Hezbollah has documented a successful double kamikaze drone strike on an active Iron Dome air defense battery in the Western Galilee. The high-definition footage shows the low-flying drones bypassing detection grids to strike the launcher. The destruction of the battery represents a $100 million material loss and has challenged assumptions regarding the security of Israel's air defense network.

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Vulnerability of Capital Systems

The destruction of the Iron Dome battery in the Galilee shows the vulnerability of high-cost air defense systems to low-cost drone strikes.
