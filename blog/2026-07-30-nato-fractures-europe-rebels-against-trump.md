# NATO Fractures: Europe Rebels Against Trump

The Director of the Russian Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR), Sergei Naryshkin, has declared that the Western alliance is undergoing a structural decoupling ("فك ارتباط"), signaling the potential collapse of NATO. Driven by opposition to the U.S. military campaign in Iran, key European nations—led by Italy, Spain, and Poland—have rebelled against Washington. The refusal of European allies to host U.S. strategic bombers, permit airspace access, or deploy air defense systems has triggered an unprecedented diplomatic crisis within the alliance.

## The Naryshkin Warning: Decoupling of the Atlantic Alliance

SVR Director Sergei Naryshkin's public assessment of the Western alliance highlights the deep divisions within NATO. Naryshkin stated that the conflict in the Middle East has acted as a solvent on the trans-Atlantic partnership.

According to Russian intelligence analysis, the decoupling is no longer a temporary diplomatic disagreement. Key European capitals view the Trump administration's unilateral military campaign against Iran as a direct threat to their energy security and economic stability, leading them to actively restrict U.S. operational access.

---

## The Moscow Paradox: Fearing an Uncontrolled Ukraine

Surprisingly, Moscow is watching the collapse of NATO with concern rather than celebration:
*   **The Fear**: Naryshkin warned that if the Atlantic alliance fractures under the weight of the Iranian war, the established rules of engagement in Ukraine will dissolve.
*   **The Escalation**: The dissolution of these rules could lead to an uncontrolled, chaotic escalation that would drag Europe and Russia into a direct conflict.
*   **The Pause**: To prevent a crisis, President Vladimir Putin has ordered a temporary freeze on major Russian offensive operations in Ukraine, only increasing troop concentrations in Sumy (سومي) as a one-month precautionary measure.

---

## The Sigonella Showdown: Italy Blocks U.S. Bombers

The most significant military disruption occurred at the Sigonella (سيغونيلا) Air Base in Sicily:
*   **The Incident**: The Italian government prohibited U.S. strategic bombers carrying munitions for the Iranian theater from landing or refueling at the base.
*   **The Covert Attempt**: U.S. commanders attempted to land the bombers covertly while they were already in the air, but Italian Defense Minister Guido Crosetto blocked the authorization.
*   **The Statement**: Crosetto declared: "Italy is not a transit airport for illegal wars." Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni distanced Rome from the campaign, labeling the unilateral U.S. strikes as unlawful.

---

## Spain's Airspace Ban: Long Loops Over the Atlantic

Following Italy's lead, Spain has enacted restrictions on U.S. military transit:
*   **Base Closure**: Madrid closed its joint-use military bases to U.S. assets deployed for the Middle East campaign.
*   **Airspace Ban**: Spain prohibited U.S. warplanes from entering its sovereign airspace.
*   **Operational Cost**: The ban has forced U.S. transport and refueling aircraft to fly in long, fuel-wasting loops over the Atlantic Ocean, wasting hours and depleting logistics reserves. Defense Minister Margarita Robles stated: "We are not participants in a unilateral war."

---

## Poland's Refusal: Keeping the Patriots on the Eastern Flank

Poland has also resisted U.S. military demands, delivering a blow to the Gulf's air defense coordination:
*   **The Request**: The Pentagon requested the deployment of Polish Patriot missile batteries to the UAE to protect forward bases from Iranian ballistic strikes.
*   **The Refusal**: Warsaw rejected the request, stating that Poland's priority remains the defense of its own airspace and NATO's eastern flank.
*   **The Vulnerability**: The decision has left U.S. installations in the Gulf exposed to Iranian missile swarms, as U.S. Patriot inventories are already depleted.

---

## Trump's Truth Social Fury: Starmer and Macron Targeted

Outraged by the lack of European support, President Trump posted a series of statements on Truth Social:
*   **Targeting London**: Trump mocked Prime Minister Keir Starmer, stating he is "no Winston Churchill" and accusing London of lacking the courage to secure the Strait of Hormuz independently.
*   **Special Relationship Threat**: He warned that "the U.S. will no longer be there to help you," threatening to dissolve the U.S.-UK Special Relationship.
*   **Targeting Paris**: Trump labeled French President Emmanuel Macron as "weak" after France restricted U.S. military overflights.

---

## The Retaliatory Embargo: Israel Bans Tech Sales to France

In response to France's airspace restrictions, Israel has intervened with a retaliatory economic measure:
*   **The Ban**: The Israeli Ministry of Defense officially banned the sale of all Israeli defense products and military technologies to France.
*   **The Irony**: The move is highly symbolic, as France was the nation that originally supplied Israel with its nuclear reactor and early military aviation technology in the 1950s.
*   **The Impact**: The embargo has disrupted joint development programs in the European defense sector, deepening the divisions between Israel and its European partners.

---

## The Clay Pot Dilemma: Europe's Security Vacuum

The rebellion against Trump has left Europe facing a security dilemma:
*   **The Clay Pot**: As Crosetto noted, Europe remains a vulnerable "clay pot" in global security, unable to defend its interests without the U.S. umbrella.
*   **The Energy Threat**: Fearing that U.S. actions will draw a counter-strike on Gulf oil installations, European finance ministers warned that no commercial airline is willing to fly over the Middle East.
*   **Strategic Isolation**: By distancing itself from Trump's war, Europe is risking the loss of U.S. military protection on its eastern border, leaving the continent isolated.

---

## Conclusion: The Fall of the Unilateral Security Umbrella

The European rebellion against President Trump's campaign in Iran marks the end of the trans-Atlantic security consensus.

By prohibiting bomber landings at Sigonella, closing airspace, and refusing to deploy Patriot batteries, Italy, Spain, and Poland have neutralized the operational cohesion of NATO. While Trump threatens to end the special relationship and Israel bans defense sales to France, the physical reality is that the U.S. can no longer wage a global war without European logistical support. The decoupling of the alliance confirms that the unilateral security umbrella is failing, forcing Europe to choose between isolation or submission to Washington's high-risk campaigns in West Asia.
