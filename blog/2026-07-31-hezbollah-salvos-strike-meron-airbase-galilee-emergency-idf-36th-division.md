# Meron Airbase Struck: Galilee Red Zone

A massive rocket and drone salvo launched by Hezbollah has struck the strategic Mount Meron Air Control Base in northern Israel. The strike has prompted Israel's Home Front Command to declare a state of emergency in Galilee, designating major border settlements as a closed military zone. The escalation coincides with heavy fighting in southern Lebanon, where the IDF's 36th Division is attempting to dislodge Hezbollah's Badr Brigade from the Ali Taher heights.

## Strategic Salvos: Hezbollah Targets Mount Meron Air Control

The air traffic control center on Mount Meron was targeted in a coordinated attack:
*   **The Strike**: Hezbollah launched over 35 rockets and drones targeting the Mount Meron base.
*   **Mount Meron**: The site serves as the primary air surveillance and defense coordination hub in the north.
*   **Detection Breach**: The salvos bypassed primary radar tracking, scoring direct hits on installation structures.

---

## The Red Zone Declaration: Galilee Declared Closed Military Area

The scale of the rocket hits forced the Home Front Command to implement emergency rules:
*   **Closed Military Zone**: The settlements of Meron, Or HaGanuz, and Safsufa have been declared closed zones.
*   **Restricted Activity**: Civil activities, including educational and work events, have been suspended.
*   **Public Safety**: Residents have been instructed to remain near protected spaces and shelter structures.

---

## Northern Command Hit: Rockets Breach Safed Defense Sectors

Secondary salvos targeted the headquarters of the Northern Command:
*   **Safed Targeted**: Rocket salvos struck administrative and security infrastructure in the city of Safed.
*   **Command Target**: The headquarters of the Northern Command was targeted in the strike path.
*   **Structural Damage**: Several military and support buildings suffered structural damage from direct hits.

---

## Iron Dome Damage: Five Batteries Silenced in Northern Salvos

The defensive screen in Galilee suffered significant losses during the engagement:
*   **Dome Hits**: Hezbollah target parameters disabled five Iron Dome launcher units.
*   **Saturated Defenses**: The high volume of low-altitude drones saturated radar tracking systems.
*   **Interception Failure**: The loss of launcher assets reduced air defense coverage across the northern sector.

---

## Tactical Advancements: The 36th Division Enters Ali Taher Heights

The rocket strikes are linked to IDF operations inside Lebanese territory:
*   **Ali Taher**: The IDF's 36th Division advanced into the strategic Ali Taher heights.
*   **Border Crossing**: The division crossed the border to establish defensive positions overlooking the Litani river.
*   **Tactical Goals**: The operation seeks to deny launch positions to mortar and rocket units.

---

## The Badr Brigade Defenses: Confronting Underground Fortifications

The advancing forces faced organized resistance from local defense units:
*   **Badr Brigade**: The sector is defended by Hezbollah's Badr Brigade.
*   **Underground Forts**: The brigade operates from tunnel networks constructed over two decades.
*   **Guerrilla Warfare**: Defense units utilized anti-tank missiles to halt the advance of armored columns.

---

## Emergency Restrictions: Safed and Katsrin Under Civil Lockdown

Civilian life in the northern Galilee and Golan heights has been restricted:
*   **Katsrin Lockdown**: The town of Katsrin has been placed under a partial civil lockdown.
*   **Assembly Limits**: Public gatherings are limited to 50 people outdoors and 200 indoors.
*   **Beach Closures**: Public beaches along the Sea of Galilee have been closed to prevent gatherings.

---

## Escalating Casualties: IDF Evacuations Under Fire near the Border

Military medical units faced challenges in evacuating wounded personnel:
*   **Border Evacuations**: Medical teams executed casualty evacuations under continuous mortar fire.
*   **Smoke Screens**: Rescue crews utilized smoke canisters to hide evacuation vehicles.
*   **Personnel Losses**: Reports indicate over 35 soldiers were wounded during the clashes in the sector.

---

## Conclusion: The Operational Collapse of the Northern Ceasefire

The strike on Mount Meron Airbase and the declaration of a state of emergency in Galilee show the collapse of the fragile ceasefire.

By targeting surveillance infrastructure and dislodging air defense batteries, Hezbollah has challenged the security of the northern border. The IDF's 36th Division's advance into the Ali Taher heights and its clashes with the Badr Brigade confirm that both sides are engaged in active conflict. As civilian centers in Safed and Katsrin adapt to lockdown parameters, these developments confirm that the border sector remains a key point of instability.
