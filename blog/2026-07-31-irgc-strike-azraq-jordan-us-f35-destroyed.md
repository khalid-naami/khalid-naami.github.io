# IRGC Strike Azraq: US F-35s Destroyed

The IRGC has launched a precision ballistic missile strike on Muwaffaq Salti Airbase in Jordan. The attack, executing the new "soldier-for-citizen" equation, targeted the U.S. forward command hub and aircraft parking ramps. According to the IRGC's "Statement No. 5," the strike completely destroyed three U.S. F-35 fighter jets on the ground, severely damaged three others, and resulted in 40 U.S. casualties.

## The Azraq Strike: Targeting the Muwaffaq Salti Command Hub

The missile attack targeted the primary U.S. military installation in Jordan:
*   **The Target**: Muwaffaq Salti Airbase (also known as Al-Azraq Airbase).
*   **The Weapon**: Emad liquid-fuel ballistic missiles with maneuverable warheads.
*   **The Rationale**: Direct retaliation for joint U.S.-Saudi airstrikes against PMF bases in Iraq.

---

## F-35 Destruction: Six Stealth Fighters Hit on the Ramps

The strike caused severe losses to U.S. advanced aviation assets:
*   **Complete Loss**: Three F-35 stealth fighters were destroyed during refueling.
*   **Severe Damage**: Three additional F-35s suffered structural damage from shrapnel.
*   **Ramp Disruption**: The explosions damaged maintenance sheds and runway surfaces.

---

## Casualty Audit: Forty U.S. Service Members Injured or Killed

The missile arrivals resulted in casualties among U.S. technical teams:
*   **The Toll**: 40 personnel were injured or killed during the attack.
*   **Ranks Affected**: Casulaties included senior engineering officers and maintenance crews.
*   **Casualty Path**: Secondary explosions at the fuel farm contributed to the toll.

---

## Traumatic Brain Injuries: The Impact of Heavy Blast Waves

Medical units have reported a rise in blast-related brain injuries among the survivors:
*   **TBI Reports**: High blast waves from heavy warheads caused traumatic brain injuries.
*   **The Symptoms**: Cognitive dysfunction and frontal lobe damage among senior personnel.
*   **Air Force Toll**: Since the conflict began, 653 U.S. soldiers have been injured, including 64 officers.

## Ali Al-Salem Base: Coordinated Strikes Destroy Kuwait Hangars

The attack was coordinated with operations targeting other U.S. logistics nodes:
*   **Kuwait Struck**: Ali Al-Salem Airbase in Kuwait was targeted by drone salvos.
*   **Hangar Loss**: Two drone maintenance hangars and a fuel farm were destroyed.
*   **Helicopter Fleet**: The strike damaged transport helicopters parked near the hangars.

## PMF Iraq Airstrikes: The Joint U.S.-Saudi Operational Cover

The escalation followed joint U.S.-Saudi operations targeting Iraqi groups:
*   **The Strike**: U.S. and Saudi aircraft targeted Popular Mobilization Forces bases.
*   **Saudi Cover**: Riyadh utilized diplomatic calls to Oman to cover the strike timeline.
*   **Tehran's Response**: Iran bypassed proxies and struck the command base in Jordan directly.

## Jordan's Denial: Amman Claims Complete Interceptions

Jordanian official media issued statements downplaying the impact of the strikes:
*   **Official Line**: Amman claimed that air defenses intercepted all incoming missiles.
*   **Satellite Evidence**: Commercial satellite images confirmed the destruction of the hangars.
*   **Public Impact**: The discrepancy challenged the credibility of local defense statements.

## U.S. Strike Freeze: Trump Suspends Planned Tehran Offensives

The destruction of the F-35s and the high casualty toll led to a halt in U.S. planning:
*   **The Decision**: Trump ordered a freeze on planned airstrikes on western Iran.
*   **Logistics Check**: Planners cited the exhaustion of Patriot interceptor stocks in Jordan.
*   **Defense Deficit**: The freeze aimed to preserve defensive capabilities for remaining bases.
