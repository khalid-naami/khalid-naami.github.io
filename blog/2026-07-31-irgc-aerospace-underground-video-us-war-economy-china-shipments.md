# IRGC Video Leak & U.S. Transition to War Economy

The strategic confrontation between the United States and Iran has intensified following a leaked video displaying the scale of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) underground missile and drone network. To counter critical munitions deficits, the Pentagon has initiated talks to transition the domestic industrial base toward a war economy, converting civilian automotive assembly lines for weapons manufacturing. Concurrently, U.S. naval forces are preparing for expanded boarding operations in international waters, citing reports of Chinese arms transfers to Tehran.

## The Underground Video Leak: Mousavi's Subterranean Tour

A leaked video from the IRGC Aerospace Force has highlighted the survival of Iran's strike capacity:
*   **The Video**: Seyed Majid Mousavi is shown inspecting long lines of cruise and ballistic missiles in underground tunnels.
*   **Mousavi's Statement**: He stated that Iran is replenishing its launch platforms at a rate exceeding pre-war levels.
*   **Industrial Resilience**: The video suggests that Iran's key assembly lines survived the initial U.S. air campaign intact.

---

## Mojtaba Khamenei's Directive: The New Offensive Era

Tehran's leadership has issued clear directives regarding the modernization of its strategic forces:
*   **Written Directive**: Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei issued a letter declaring the expansion of defensive and offensive systems as a top priority.
*   **No De-escalation**: The message signals that Iran will not accept limitations on its missile range or payload capacity.
*   **Unified Command**: The directive aligns with statements from both reformist and conservative factions within the government.

## Propaganda or Material Fact: Reuters and Western Dilemma

Western intelligence agencies are analyzing the authenticity of the underground footage:
*   **Verification Difficulty**: Reuters reported that independent verification of the underground installations is not currently possible.
*   **Two Hypotheses**: Analysts are divided on whether the video shows a functional strategic reserve or serves as a deterrence display.
*   **Industrial Fact**: Even if partially staged, the presence of modern assembly tools indicates that domestic production continues.

## The U.S. War Economy: Auto Plants Convert to Arms Production

The Pentagon is taking steps to restructure domestic manufacturing to address supply shortages:
*   **Early Talks**: The Department of Defense has initiated discussions with major automakers, including General Motors and Ford.
*   **Assembly Line Conversion**: The plan involves converting parts of civilian car factories to produce military hardware.
*   **Focus Areas**: Auto plants would be tasked with assembling chassis, rocket bodies, and electronic sub-assemblies.

---

## The Munich Deficit: Strategic Stockpiles Plunge Thirty-Seven Percent

The U.S. military faces resource constraints due to parallel security commitments:
*   **The Stockpile Drop**: U.S. reserves of key interceptors and cruise missiles have decreased by approximately 37%.
*   **Diverted Shipments**: The Pentagon has redirected ordnance originally allocated for European allies to the Middle East.
*   **Production Bottlenecks**: Standard defense contractors are operating at capacity, requiring the integration of civilian industries.

---

## Chinese Arms Accusations: The Rationale for Ship Seizures

Washington and Tel Aviv have accused Beijing of supplying military hardware to Tehran:
*   **The Accusation**: Reports claim that China is transferring electronic components and guidance systems to Iran.
*   **Justifying the Blockade**: Analysts suggest the reports are used to justify boarding operations against Iranian shipping.
*   **Chinese Denial**: Beijing has stated that its trade with Iran consists of normal commercial and industrial goods.

---

## Interception Protocols: Boarding Orders for Commercial Ships

The U.S. Navy has updated its rules of engagement for enforcing the shipping blockade:
*   **The Protocol**: Navy vessels are authorized to intercept and board commercial ships suspected of carrying restricted goods.
*   **Forceful Measures**: The interception of the cargo ship *Toska* showed the U.S. is prepared to use disabling fire to enforce compliance.
*   **Seizure Orders**: Boarded vessels and their cargoes are subject to detention in international waters.

---

## Trump's Strategic Setup: Muwaffaq al-Salti Radar and Pakistan

The U.S. is adjusting its regional defense networks to prepare for potential escalation:
*   **Radar Deployment**: A new early warning radar was deployed at Muwaffaq al-Salti Air Base in Jordan to replace damaged systems.
*   **Air Defense Boost**: Interceptor batteries in Kuwait and the UAE have been reinforced with fresh missile supplies.
*   **Pakistan-Saudi Pact**: The U.S. is seeking to activate joint defense agreements, placing Pakistani forces on Saudi soil to deter retaliation.

---

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Standoff of Domestic Resilience

The current phase of the conflict highlights the contrast between the industrial capacities of the two sides.

While the U.S. must transition to a war economy and redirect global stockpiles to sustain its operations, Iran's underground video suggests it maintains domestic assembly lines. The U.S. strategy of intercepting commercial ships, based on claims of Chinese arms transfers, seeks to cut off external support. However, as long as Iran retains the capacity to assemble systems domestically and enforce the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, the U.S. face a resilient adversary that cannot be easily isolated by naval blockades.
