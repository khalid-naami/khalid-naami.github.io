# The Clash of Civilizations: Dugin's Stance

Russian presidential press secretary Dmitry Peskov has declared that the "special military operation" in Ukraine has officially transformed into a "real war" due to the direct involvement of the collective West. In an interview on Sputnik TV, philosopher Alexander Dugin expanded on this, declaring the transition to a global "Civilizational War." Dugin argued that the era of localized technical operations has ended, requiring the mobilization of the Moscow-Tehran-Beijing axis to challenge Western hegemony.

## The War Declaration: Peskov Defines the Clash with the Collective West

The Kremlin has adjusted its official definition of the conflict in Eastern Europe:
*   **Official War**: Dmitry Peskov declared that the campaign has transitioned into a real war.
*   **Western Staging**: The shift is attributed to the active participation of NATO and Washington.
*   **Policy Shift**: The declaration marks a formal recognition that localized containment has failed.

---

## Dugin's Philosophy: Sputnik Interview Outlines the Civilizational Standoff

Philosopher Alexander Dugin discussed the strategic meaning of the Kremlin's declaration:
*   **Civilizational War**: Dugin defined the conflict as a struggle between civilizational models.
*   **End of Coexistence**: The philosopher argued that integration into the Western order is no longer possible.
*   **Unified Front**: The standoff requires a coordinated response from non-Western powers.

---

## The Russia-Iran-China Axis: Coordinating Against Western Hegemony

The transition has accelerated the integration of the three primary challenger states:
*   **Strategic Axis**: Moscow, Tehran, and Beijing are coordinating security and economic paths.
*   **Shared Burden**: The states share tracking data, economic networks, and military logistics.
*   **Multipolar Order**: The coalition seeks to establish secure alternatives to Western financial systems.

---

## Special Operations End: The Failure of Localized Technical Interventions

Dugin noted that the initial expectations of localized containment campaigns were unrealistic:
*   **Kyiv Campaign**: The failure to capture Kyiv in May 2022 ended the localized phase.
*   **Hostomel Standoff**: The battle near the capital demonstrated that Kiev would receive full Western support.
*   **Hormuz Context**: Similarly, the U.S. attempts to manage the Gulf via limited strikes have failed.

---

## Wartime Crisis Managers: Replacing Peacetime Elites in Russia and Iran

The transition to a civilizational war requires structural changes within the challenger states:
*   **New Cadres**: Dugin called for replacing peacetime elites with wartime crisis managers.
*   **Flexibility and Boldness**: The new cadres must be capable of managing continuous escalations.
*   **Tehran Integration**: Iran is implementing similar changes, placing military figures in administrative posts.

## Western Sanctions Cuba: China Joins the Condemnation Campaign

Beijing has expanded its diplomatic support for states facing U.S.-led economic restrictions:
*   **Cuba Sanctions**: China issued statements condemning the ongoing blockade of Cuba.
*   **Economic Front**: The statements link the Cuban sanctions to the embargoes on Russia and Iran.
*   **Coalition Push**: China is leveraging these issues to build broader coalitions at the United Nations.

## Tactical Stagnation: The Awakening of Legacy Military Structures

Dugin criticized the slow adaptation of military bureaucracies to wartime realities:
*   **Bureaucratic Sleep**: The philosopher noted that military command structures adjust slowly.
*   **Performance Demand**: The transition requires proving capabilities on the ground rather than relying on status.
*   **Logistics Stress**: Sustained campaigns have tested the production limits of industrial complexes.

## The Nuclear Horizon: Deterrence as the Final Defensive Line

The civilizational standoff has placed strategic deterrent systems at the center of planning:
*   **Nuclear Shield**: Russia's nuclear capacity provides a shield for the broader alliance.
*   **Iran Nuclear Status**: Tehran's enrichment programs are viewed as a necessary step for defense parity.
*   **Integrated Shield**: The axis is coordinating deterrence protocols to prevent unilateral Western strikes.

## Conclusion: The Structural Reality of a Multipolar Standoff

Dmitry Peskov's war declaration and Alexander Dugin's Sputnik interview show the structural reality of a multipolar standoff.

By declaring the transition to a civilizational war and acknowledging the limits of localized special operations, Russian planners have aligned their rhetoric with geopolitical realities. The integration of the Russia-Iran-China axis and the call for wartime crisis managers confirm that the confrontation with the collective West has entered a persistent phase. As both blocs adjust their structures, these developments show that regional conflicts are now parts of a global civilizational divide.
