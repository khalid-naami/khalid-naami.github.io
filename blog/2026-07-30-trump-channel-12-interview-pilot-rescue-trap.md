# Trump Channel 12: Pilot Rescue as Decoy Trap

In a segment broadcast by Israel's Channel 12, President Donald Trump provided new details about the rescue of the downed F-15 aircrew in Isfahan. Trump admitted that U.S. command initially feared a radio message sent from the second pilot's device was an "Iranian trap." The message, which bypassed standard military security codes, stated "God is Good" (translated to Hebrew as *Elohim Hu Tov*). Analysts and Iranian media now confirm that the pilot was indeed used as a tactical decoy by the IRGC, resulting in a costly operation.

## The Channel 12 Interview: Trump Reveals Code Dilemma

President Trump discussed the tactical details of the second pilot's recovery with reporter Barak Ravid:
*   **The Admission**: Trump revealed that the second pilot's rescue was executed in broad daylight under heavy fire—an operational choice the U.S. military typically avoids.
*   **The Code Breach**: The pilot sent a transmission that did not follow the established daily security codes, immediately raising alarms at command headquarters.
*   **The Verification**: Trump claimed that command verified the pilot's identity only because his colleagues confirmed he was highly religious.

## The \"God is Good\" Broadcast: Decoy or Delirium?

The transmission sent from the pilot's radio unit bypassed standard security procedures:
*   **The Transmission**: The pilot's device broadcasted the phrase "God is Good" (or "Allah Karim" in the regional context).
*   **The Threat Assessment**: Command officers suspected the IRGC had captured the pilot and was using his radio to lure U.S. aircraft into an active combat zone.
*   **The Deviation**: By transmitting religious phrases instead of structured alphanumeric codes, the signal acted as a double-edged beacon.

## The Hebrew Translation: Linguistic Inconsistencies

The translation of the pilot's transmission highlights the complexity of the communications:
*   **Ravid's Translation**: In his reporting for Channel 12, Barak Ravid translated the phrase to Hebrew as *Elohim Hu Tov* (God is Good).
*   **Linguistic Discrepancy**: In traditional Hebrew theological contexts, this literal translation is rarely used; terms like *Rachum* (Merciful) or *Channun* (Gracious) are standard.
*   **The Origin**: The mismatch suggests that the transmission was translated back and forth across different languages during the command chain, adding to the initial confusion.

## The Concussed Pilot: Logical Flaws in the 12-Km Walk

The official U.S. narrative contains significant logical contradictions regarding the pilot's movement:
*   **The Claim**: The Pentagon asserts that the pilot managed to traverse 10 to 12 kilometers of hostile terrain to reach a secure crevice.
*   **The Injury**: Israeli intelligence confirmed the pilot had suffered a severe brain concussion and multiple fractures during ejection.
*   **The Paradox**: A soldier suffering from a severe concussion and physical trauma cannot walk 12 kilometers through contested zones—indicating he was moved by other forces.

## The IRGC Trap: Using the Wounded Pilot as Bait

Iranian military analysts present a different version of the events surrounding the rescue:
*   **The Finding**: IRGC units located the pilot in a critically injured, near-fatal state.
*   **The Hospital Factor**: Because U.S. air strikes had damaged local medical centers, the Iranians could not provide the advanced treatment needed to keep him alive.
*   **The Decoy**: Rather than let him die in their custody, the IRGC placed the pilot in the crevice and used his radio to transmit the un-coded signal. This turned him into a beacon to draw U.S. extraction teams into an ambush.

## The First Pilot's Fate: Where is the Survivor?

While the U.S. focuses on the second pilot, the fate of the first pilot remains unconfirmed:
*   **The Rescue Claim**: The U.S. asserts the first pilot was rescued during the initial seven-hour ground battle.
*   **No Documentation**: No photographs, video footage, or official statements from the first pilot have been released.
*   **The Suspicion**: The total lack of visual evidence raises questions about whether the first pilot survived the engagement or remains in critical condition.

## Operational Failures: Stranded Aircraft and Heavy Fire

The rescue mission resulted in severe material and human losses for U.S. special operations:
*   **Stranded Transports**: Two MC-130J transport aircraft became stuck in sandy terrain and were subsequently destroyed by U.S. forces to prevent technology capture.
*   **Stranded Troops**: Over 80 special operations commandos were left without an evacuation route, requiring a secondary rescue run.
*   **Casualties**: Leaks confirm that over 50 U.S. soldiers were wounded or killed during the 48-hour operation.
