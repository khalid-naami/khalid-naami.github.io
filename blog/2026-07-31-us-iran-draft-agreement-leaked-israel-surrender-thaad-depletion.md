# US-Iran Draft Treaty: Israel Decries Surrender

A leaked draft of the final U.S.-Iran agreement has generated criticism in Israel, where media outlets describe the terms as a "U.S. surrender to Iran." The draft follows reports of significant U.S. air defense stockpile depletion and a strategic shift by Gulf states toward China for munitions. The agreement establishes a framework that halts maritime blockades while restricting Israeli access to the Persian Gulf, ending major regional economic projects.

## THAAD Interceptor Depletion: The Washington Post Inventory Audit

The pressure to finalize the agreement is linked to constraints in air defense inventories:
*   **The Audit**: *The Washington Post* reported that the U.S. used 50% of its global THAAD interceptor stocks during the conflict.
*   **Israel's Reserves**: The report criticized Israel for hoarder behavior, retaining its Arrow interceptor stocks while relying on U.S. assets.
*   **Inventory Limit**: Planners warned that a second 39-day conflict would reduce U.S. interceptor stocks to zero.

*   **Asymmetric Strength**: The rapid recovery confirms that the air campaign did not eliminate Iran's tactical strike capacity.

*   **Strategic Independence**: The pivot to Beijing reduces Gulf dependence on U.S. logistics pipelines.

## Ten Key Clauses: Analyzing the Draft Agreement Terms

The draft document outlines a comprehensive framework to stabilize relations:
*   **Ceasefire**: An immediate, unconditional ceasefire across land, sea, and air sectors.
*   **Non-Aggression**: Both sides agree not to strike military, civilian, or economic installations.
*   **Hormuz Transit**: Guarantees for freedom of navigation in the Gulf and Strait of Hormuz under the 1982 Law of the Sea.
*   **Sovereignty**: Mutual respect for territorial integrity and non-interference in internal affairs.

*   **Sanctions Relief**: U.S. secondary sanctions will be lifted in phases as Iran complies with enrichment limits.

## Collapsing the Abrahamic Corridors: IMEC Transit Deletion

*   **IMEC Collapse**: The India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) has been effectively cancelled.
*   **Abraham Accords**: The "Abrahamic economic integration" between the Gulf and Israel has been suspended.
*   **Shipping Diversion**: Israeli merchant vessels are barred from the Gulf, forcing them to route around Africa.

## Conclusion: The New Security Equilibrium in the Persian Gulf

The leaked U.S.-Iran draft treaty represents a significant change in the security dynamics of the Middle East.

As Senator Rubio urges caution, the agreement confirms that future security arrangements will be defined by local balances of power rather than external guarantees.
