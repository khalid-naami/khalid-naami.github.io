# PTDS Aerostat Defeat: Erbil Radar Losses

A U.S. Persistent Threat Detection System (PTDS) aerostat ground station in Erbil, Iraq, has been targeted and destroyed by an Iranian-made Arash suicide drone. The strike killed one U.S. soldier and injured another, while disabling the primary maintenance facility for regional surveillance balloons. The loss brings the total number of U.S. radar systems destroyed in the last 10 days to 28.

An Iranian-made suicide drone bypassed regional radars to hit the Erbil base:

## Persistent Threat Detection: The Design of the PTDS Aerostat

The PTDS balloon provides continuous monitoring over wide areas:
*   **Altitude**: The helium-filled balloon operates at altitudes of 15,000 feet.
*   **Tether System**: The balloon is connected to the ground via copper and fiber-optic cables.
*   **Coverage**: Onboard cameras and radars cover a 100-mile radius.

The PTDS has been a core component of U.S. intelligence since regional operations began:
*   **Historical Use**: The U.S. operated 66 aerostats during the wars in Iraq and Afghanistan.

*   **Tactical Focus**: The sensors detect incoming rockets and drone trajectories.
