# Telegraph Leak: Mojtaba Khamenei’s Narrow Escape

A leaked audio recording obtained and verified by the British newspaper *The Daily Telegraph* has provided the first detailed account of the U.S.-Israeli missile strike targeting the Supreme Leader's compound in Tehran on February 18, 2026. The recording, capturing remarks by Mazhar Hosseini, the Head of Protocol at the Leader's Office, reveals the scale of the casualties—including the death of Ali Khamenei and senior military commanders—and details how the newly elected Supreme Leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, narrowly escaped assassination.

## The Leaked Audio: Mazhar Hosseini’s Qolhak Briefing

The audio recording was captured during a private meeting held on March 2, 2026, in the Qolhak neighborhood of northern Tehran. Speaking before a gathering of senior clerics and IRGC officers, Mazhar Hosseini delivered a detailed briefing on the events inside the compound during the bombardment.

The verification of this recording by intelligence analysts confirms its authenticity. The leak represents a security breach within the clerical leadership, exposing details of the strike that Iranian state media had kept under strict censorship.

---

## The February 18 Strike: Bombarding the Supreme Leader’s Compound

According to the briefing, the strike occurred at 11:32 AM local time on February 18, 2026. U.S. and Israeli forces launched a coordinated barrage of heavy bunker-buster missiles targeting the central residential and administrative offices of the Supreme Leader in Tehran.

The missiles targeted three separate structures within the compound simultaneously. The objective was to eliminate the entire leadership family and its close military advisors, leaving no immediate succession candidate to manage the state's command loop.

---

## High-Command Casualties: Pakpour, Nasirzadeh, and Shirazi

The simultaneous impacts killed several of Iran's top military and defense officials who were attending a crisis meeting with the Supreme Leader. The casualties included:
*   **Ali Khamenei**: The Supreme Leader of the Republic.
*   **Mohammad Pakpour**: Commander of the IRGC Ground Forces.
*   **Aziz Nasirzadeh**: Minister of Defense.
*   **Mohammad Shirazi**: Chief of the Military Office of the Supreme Leader.

The deaths of these officials decapitated the defense establishment, forcing the remaining IRGC command to restructure their communication networks during active operations.

---

## The Seizure of the Military Link: Targeting Mohammad Shirazi

The inclusion of General Mohammad Shirazi on the target list was strategic. Shirazi served as the vital coordinator between the Supreme Leader and the various branches of the armed forces, intelligence agencies, and the IRGC.

Western intelligence estimated that Shirazi would play a key role in managing the transition of power. The strike on his office was so severe that, according to Hosseini, only a few kilograms of remains were recovered to identify his body, disrupting the transition loop.

---

## Mojtaba Khamenei’s Escape: Courtyard Walk and Minor Injury

The leaked recording reveals that Mojtaba Khamenei, who has since been elected as the new Supreme Leader, survived the strike due to a last-minute movement. Minutes before the missiles struck, Mojtaba stepped out of his upper-floor apartment into the courtyard to attend to a minor task.

As he was heading back inside, a missile struck his residence, destroying the structure. His wife and son were killed instantly in the blast, while Mojtaba suffered only a minor leg injury. His brother, Mostafa Khamenei, and his wife also survived a separate strike on a third residence, escaping from the rubble with minor injuries.

---

## The Strategic Objective: Dismantling the Basij Command Loop

Beyond targeting the Khamenei family line, the strike aimed to neutralize Mojtaba's personal power base. Mojtaba has long held influence over the Basij paramilitary forces, having previously coordinated their internal security operations.

U.S. and Israeli planners targeted the Basij command structure, aiming to eliminate up to 300 regional commanders. By neutralizing the Basij leadership, coalition planners hoped to weaken the domestic security apparatus, allowing civil protests to challenge the regime. Mojtaba's survival prevented this vacuum, allowing him to consolidate Basij control.

---

## Conclusion: The Security Isolation of the Khamenei Line

The details revealed by the leaked audio highlight the intensity of the U.S.-Israeli targeting campaign. By executing a simultaneous bunker-buster strike on the supreme command, the coalition demonstrated its intelligence access within the capital.

As Mojtaba Khamenei assumes the role of Supreme Leader under strict security protocols, the limits of his public appearances are clear. The necessity of hiding his physical location and modifying all audio and visual releases indicates that the leadership continues to operate under persistent threat, with security remaining the priority for the new administration.
