# Netanyahu Expels France from Lebanon Ceasefire Talks

In a significant diplomatic escalation, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has barred France from participating in direct talks between Israel and Lebanon scheduled to begin in Washington. The move comes as U.S. President Donald Trump applies maximum pressure on Israel to de-escalate strikes on Hezbollah. In response, Iran's Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf has laid down two strict preconditions before starting the broader Islamabad peace summit: a comprehensive ceasefire in Lebanon and the immediate release of frozen Iranian assets.

## ## Ghalibaf's Ultimatum: Two Iranian Pre-conditions

Iran has asserted its requirements before committing to the Islamabad peace process:
*   **The Ultimatum**: Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf declared that no negotiations will proceed in Pakistan without prior compliance with core conditions.
*   **Lebanon Ceasefire**: Fulfilling a complete halt to U.S.-backed Israeli military operations in Lebanon.
*   **Asset Release**: The immediate unfreezing of all Iranian assets held in international banking systems.

---

## ## Desperate Maneuvers: Netanyahu's Tense Calls with Trump

Netanyahu's recent diplomatic moves were driven by pressure from the White House:
*   **The Tense Calls**: CNN reported three tense phone calls between Trump and Netanyahu prior to the announcement.
*   **Unilateral Threat**: Trump warned that if Israel did not initiate talks, the U.S. would declare a unilateral ceasefire.
*   **De-escalation Demands**: The president pressured Netanyahu to reduce the volume of airstrikes in Beirut and southern Lebanon.

---

## ## Direct Talks: Separating Lebanon from the Iranian Front

Israel is attempting to resolve the Lebanese theater through a separate bilateral channel:
*   **The Separation**: Netanyahu announced direct negotiations with the Lebanese government, aiming to bypass the multi-front ceasefire framework.
*   **Disarmament Focus**: The Israeli agenda focuses on disarming Hezbollah and organizing peaceful border relations.
*   **Iranian Refusal**: Tehran has rejected this separation, insisting that any ceasefire must cover all resistance fronts simultaneously.

---

## ## Ambassador Channels: Leter and Nada Hamade Moawad

The direct talks will be managed at the diplomatic level in Washington:
*   **The Teams**: Negotiations will be led by Israel's Ambassador to the U.S. (Leter/Leiter) and Lebanese Ambassador Nada Hamade Moawad.
*   **Simulated Structure**: The talks are scheduled to begin early next week, ahead of the formal opening of the Islamabad summit.
*   **The Objective**: Establishing a direct communication line to bypass international mediation and manage border disputes.

---

## ## Sidelining Dermer: Netanyahu Reorganizes the Team

The diplomatic shift has resulted in structural changes within Israel's security team:
*   **Dermer Sidelined**: Minister Ron Dermer was removed from the active negotiation channel.
*   **Diverging Views**: Dermer had supported a comprehensive, U.S.-managed ceasefire across all fronts, which Netanyahu opposed.
*   **Military Backing**: The sidelining of Dermer was supported by the IDF command, which sought to maintain the option of continuing ground operations.

---

## ## Expelling France: The Washington Negotiation Clash

Netanyahu has officially excluded French diplomats from participating in the Washington talks:
*   **The Expulsion**: According to the *Jerusalem Post*, Israel rejected any French role in the direct Israel-Lebanon channel.
*   **No Honest Broker**: Israeli officials accused President Emmanuel Macron's administration of bias, citing French calls for arms restrictions.
*   **Airspace Restriction**: The decision follows France's refusal to permit U.S. military transport aircraft carrying weapons to Israel to cross its airspace.

---

## ## Sidelining Spain: Diplomatic Isolation in Krayat Kad

Spain has also been excluded from the monitoring and observer networks:
*   **Krayat Kad Exclusion**: Spanish military observers were expelled from monitoring stations in southern Israel (Krayat Kad).
*   **European Friction**: The exclusions have deepened the rift between Tel Aviv and European capitals.
*   **Growing Isolation**: Even traditional allies like Germany and Italy have criticized Netanyahu's unilateral diplomatic expulsions.

---

## ## Naim Qassem's Warning: No Futile Concessions

Hezbollah's leadership has warned the Lebanese government against accepting unfavorable terms:
*   **Qassem's Statement**: Secretary-General Naim Qassem declared that the group will not accept a return to the pre-war status quo.
*   **No Concessions**: Qassem urged Lebanese negotiators to halt "futile concessions" that compromise national defense.
*   **Defense Ready**: The group warned that its rocket and naval cruise missile systems remain fully prepared to resume strikes.

---

## ## Conclusion: Israel's Unprecedented Diplomatic Isolation

The decision by Netanyahu to expel France and Spain from the Lebanon ceasefire negotiations has left Israel increasingly isolated.

While the direct talks in Washington between Ambassador Leter and Nada Hamade Moawad seek to bypass regional agreements, the Iranian preconditions of a unified ceasefire and asset release remain unresolved. Netanyahu's sidelining of Ron Dermer and his public dispute with Trump reveal a widening rift with Israel's primary ally. As European states restrict military logistics and Hezbollah refuses to accept unilateral disarmament, Israel faces a choice between a prolonged war of attrition and accepting the comprehensive terms of the Islamabad summit.
