# Israeli F-15 Hit: Rescue Helicopter Accident

An Israeli Air Force F-15 fighter jet has suffered damage from a surface-to-air missile (SAM) launched during operations over southern Lebanon. While military spokespersons officially reported "no damage," sources confirmed the damaged jet triggered emergency alerts near the Atlit naval base. Concurrently, a rescue operation to evacuate wounded Golani Brigade soldiers experienced a significant accident when a loose thermal emergency blanket was sucked into a helicopter's engine, disabling the aircraft.

## SAM Engagement: Israeli F-15 Targeted Over Southern Lebanon

Israeli combat aircraft faced direct air defense fire during border patrol missions:
*   **The Attack**: Hezbollah air defense units launched a surface-to-air missile targeting an IAF formation.
*   **The Target**: The missile engaged an F-15 fighter jet operating within the combat zone.
*   **Direct Hit**: The close proximity of the missile's detonation damaged the aircraft's propulsion systems.

---

## Sirens in Atlit: Alerts Near Strategic Gas and Naval Positions

The engagement triggered emergency alarms in coastal defense sectors:
*   **Atlit Sirens**: Rocket and air defense alerts were activated in the Nif'am/Atlit coastal sector.
*   **Strategic Facilities**: The alerts occurred near the offshore gas extraction platform and the Atlit naval base.
*   **Civilian Concern**: The activation of sirens in these sectors caused local concern regarding the safety of energy infrastructure.

## Emergency Landing Sequence: Grounding the Damaged F-15 Fighter

The pilot managed to guide the damaged jet back to a military airfield:
*   **The Landing**: The F-15 declared an emergency and executed a rapid landing sequence.
*   **Systems Damage**: Ground audits confirmed damage to the engine exhaust and rear fuselage panels.
*   **Flight Suspension**: The aircraft has been removed from active rotation for structural repairs.

---

## Helicopter Accident: Rescue Rotors Draw Thermal Blankets

An unusual accident occurred during medical evacuation operations in the eastern sector:
*   **The Evacuation**: Two helicopters landed in southern Lebanon to evacuate three wounded Golani Brigade soldiers.
*   **The Blanket**: Medics wrapped a wounded soldier in a thin aluminum thermal emergency blanket.
*   **Loose Gear**: The blanket was not properly secured to the stretcher before take-off.

---

## Engine Failure: Aluminum Foil Accident Disables Evacuation Flight

The loose metallic sheet was drawn into the propulsion systems of the landing helicopter:
*   **The Intake**: Wind from the rotors blew the thin aluminum sheet directly into the helicopter's engine intake.
*   **Compressor Stall**: The metal sheet caused a compressor stall, disabling the engine.
*   **Flight Grounded**: The helicopter was grounded at the landing site, requiring a secondary rescue team.

---

## Ground Casualties: Golani Brigade Reservists Evacuated Under Fire

The evacuation followed drone strikes on forward infantry positions:
*   **The Strike**: Hezbollah kamikaze drones hit an infantry squad operating near the border.
*   **The Wounded**: Three Golani Brigade reservists suffered shrapnel injuries during the attack.
*   **Second Flight**: A backup helicopter successfully evacuated the wounded to the Galilee Medical Center.

## Command Coordination: The 36th Division Evacuation Protocol

The ground operations in the eastern sector are managed by the 36th Division:
*   **The Protocol**: Evacuation procedures require coordinate movements between ground units and air assets.
*   **Logistical Strain**: The double accident—the F-15 hit and the helicopter grounding—strained local logistics.
*   **Security Review**: The division has initiated a review of loose equipment protocols during active operations.

## Escalating Confrontations: Hezbollah's Twenty-Two Weekend Operations

The incidents occurred during a weekend of intense tactical activity:
*   **Twenty-Two Operations**: Hezbollah executed 22 separate operations targeting border positions.
*   **Low Altitude Attacks**: The operations utilized low-altitude kamikaze drones to strike command posts.
*   **Air Cover Limits**: The intense activity has limited the availability of close air support for ground units.

The damage to the Israeli F-15 and the grounding of the rescue helicopter show the operational challenges facing border operations.
