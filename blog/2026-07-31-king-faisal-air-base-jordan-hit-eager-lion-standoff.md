# King Faisal Base Strike: Eager Lion Standoff

Iran has expanded its offensive against U.S. staging grounds in Jordan, launching a precision missile strike on King Faisal Air Base (Al-Jafr) in the southern province of Ma'an. The strike targeted joint command structures and logistics networks utilized for the U.S.-led "Eager Lion" military exercises. By disabling integrated radar networks, the attack has compromised the Levant air defense buffer that protects Israel's eastern border.

## The Third Airbase Hit: King Faisal Base Struck in Ma'an

The Iranian campaign has systematically targeted the network of airbases supporting U.S. forces in Jordan:
*   **The Strike**: King Faisal Air Base was targeted by a heavy ballistic missile salvo.
*   **Third Installation**: The facility is the third Jordanian airbase struck, following Al-Azraq and H-5.
*   **Southern Sector**: The attack expands the geographical reach of the strikes to southern Jordan.

The target is an airbase with historical links to regional defense funding:

*   **Saudi Funding**: The project was funded by Saudi Arabia's King Faisal bin Abdulaziz.
*   **Platform History**: The base has historically hosted F-5 training detachments.
