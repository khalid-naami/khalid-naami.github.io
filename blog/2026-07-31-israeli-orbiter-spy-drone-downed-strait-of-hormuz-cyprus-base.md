# Israeli Orbiter Spy Drone Downed in Hormuz

The regular Iranian Navy has recovered the wreckage of an Israeli spy drone shot down over the Strait of Hormuz. The drone, identified as an Orbiter reconnaissance platform, was launched from a base in Cyprus to monitor defensive installations near Bandar Abbas. While the IDF claimed no knowledge of the incident, the downing highlights the strategic limitations faced by Israeli intelligence following the reduction of its domestic assets in Iran.
