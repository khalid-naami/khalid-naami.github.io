# Kheibar Shekan Missiles Paralyze Israeli Airbases

Iran has bypassed quantitative saturation tactics by launching exactly 20 high-precision Kheibar Shekan ballistic missiles targeting Israel's airbase triad. The strikes, divided among Ramat David, Tel Nof, and Nevatim bases, achieved an estimated 80% to 95% impact rate. The missiles' maneuvering warheads bypassed Israel's layered air defense network, including the Arrow, David's Sling, and newly deployed U.S. THAAD systems.

## The Precision Salvo: Twenty Kheibar Shekan Ballistic Missiles

Tehran has selected a qualitative approach for its missile strikes on strategic aviation centers:
*   **The Salvo**: A precise wave of 20 Kheibar Shekan missiles was launched from western Iran.
*   **Target Selection**: The targets were limited to the three primary military airbases.
*   **Zero Decoys**: The strike relied on the maneuverability of the warheads rather than massive decoy salvos.

---

## Paralyzing the Triad: Target Audits of Ramat David, Tel Nof, and Nevatim

The strike plan targeted the bases responsible for launching deep-theater operations:
*   **Ramat David**: The northern base suffered strikes on its main flight command structure.
*   **Tel Nof**: Central hangars and support facilities sustained direct hits.
*   **Nevatim**: The southern F-35 base experienced impacts near hangar and repair bays.

## Technical Specifications: Fuel Systems and Warhead Configurations

The Kheibar Shekan is a third-generation solid-fuel medium-range ballistic missile:
*   **Solid Fuel**: The solid-propellant motor enables rapid launch preparation within 20 minutes.
*   **Warhead**: The missile carries a warhead containing 500 to 600 kg of high explosives.
*   **Maneuvering Reentry**: The warhead separates and maneuvers during the final descent phase.

---

## Mach Three Impact: High-Velocity Entry Speeds and Launch Protocols

The missile achieves high velocities during its terminal descent to bypass interceptors:
*   **Velocity**: The warhead hits speeds of Mach 3 during the final descent phase.
*   **Mobile Launchers**: The missiles are launched from mobile transporters disguised as civilian vehicles.
*   **Reaction Time**: The combination of solid fuel and mobile launchers limits early detection opportunities.

## Paralyzed Air Defenses: Bypassing the Arrow Two and Arrow Three Screens

Israel's upper-tier Exoatmospheric defense screens failed to intercept the maneuvering targets:
*   **Arrow Interceptors**: Arrow-2 and Arrow-3 batteries fired multiple interceptors during the descent.
*   **Maneuvering Defeat**: The warheads executed lateral maneuvers, breaking the tracking locks.
*   **Zero Intercepts**: Upper-tier batteries recorded no verified intercepts of the first-wave missiles.

---

## David's Sling Failure: Mid-Altitude Defense Systems Saturated

The mid-tier defense network was unable to establish intercept coordinates:
*   **David's Sling**: Mid-altitude batteries failed to track the warheads' changing trajectories.
*   **Calculations Deficit**: The processing computers were unable to calculate the intercept coordinates in time.
*   **Base Impacts**: The failure allowed the warheads to reach their target bases without mid-tier interception.

---

## THAAD Battle Assessment: U.S. Terminal Battery Performance

The newly deployed U.S. terminal defense battery faced challenges in tracking the warheads:
*   **THAAD Battery**: The U.S. THAAD system operated alongside local defense units.
*   **Terminal Tracking**: The system's radar tracked the warheads but struggled to establish firing solutions.
*   **Integration Limits**: The lack of integration with local mid-tier systems limited intercept performance.

---

## Delaying the Response: The Operational Impact of Base Strikes

The damage to airbase infrastructure restricted Israel's immediate retaliatory options:
*   **Delayed Response**: The hits on Ramat David delayed air operations until the morning.
*   **Refueling Limits**: The targeting of support equipment limited long-range flight operations.
*   **Parality**: The temporary loss of base capabilities forced a reliance on external naval assets.

## Conclusion: The Strategic Implications of Terminal Maneuvering Warheads

The precision strike by Kheibar Shekan ballistic missiles and the failure of the Arrow, David's Sling, and THAAD systems show the limits of layered air defense networks.

By utilizing solid-fuel propulsion and terminal maneuvering warheads, the salvos bypassed Exoatmospheric and terminal interceptors to paralyze the airbase triad. The operational delay of retaliatory flights and the damage to Nevatim and Tel Nof show the strategic impact of these weapons. As military planners review the performance of tracking systems, these developments confirm that maneuvering reentry technologies present new challenges to conventional theater defense screens.
