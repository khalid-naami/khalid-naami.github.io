# General Yitzhak Brick: Israel Existential Threat

Retired IDF Major General Yitzhak Brick has published an assessment in *Maariv* warning that Israel faces an existential threat that could impact its long-term stability. Brick's warnings focus on the depletion of the air force, the re-organization of Hamas in Gaza, and the emergence of a strategic alliance between Egypt and Turkey. He argues that these developments have challenged the core assumptions of Israeli security planning.

## The Prophet of Wrath: General Brick Warns of Existential Collapse

The retired general has warned of structural vulnerabilities within the state's security apparatus:
*   **Existential Warnings**: Brick asserted that the combination of political divisions and military strain has weakened the state.
*   **The Centennial Risk**: He warned that without structural changes in leadership, the state may struggle to reach its 100th anniversary.
*   **Demanding Reforms**: Brick called for a revision of the national security concept to prepare for multi-theater conflicts.

---

## The IDF Secrets Audit: Grounding Two Fighter Squadrons

The report details high wear-and-tear rates among the air force's primary combat platforms:
*   **Grounded Jets**: Classified audits indicate that approximately 24 to 26 fighter jets (F-15s and F-35s) have been grounded.
*   **Maintenance Backlogs**: The grounding is due to excessive flight hours accumulated during the conflict and shortages of replacement parts.
*   **Operational Readiness**: The loss of these platforms has reduced the air force's capacity to execute long-range operations.

---

## Gaza Realities: Intelligence Confirms Hamas's Re-organization

Internal military assessments confirm that operations in Gaza have not dismantled the local administration:
*   **Re-organizing**: A leaked intelligence document confirms that Hamas is rebuilding its civil and military networks in Gaza.
*   **Rapid Recruitment**: The organization is recruiting new personnel and re-establishing civil control in areas cleared by the military.
*   **Policy Failure**: Brick argued that relying on temporary raids without a political transition plan has allowed the group to persist.

---

## The MIRV Failures: Inability to Intercept Split Warheads

Defense systems faced technical challenges during recent missile attacks:
*   **MIRV Attacks**: Iranian forces deployed ballistic missiles equipped with Multiple Independently Targetable Reentry Vehicles (MIRV).
*   **Intercept Gaps**: Air defense batteries struggled to track and intercept the separate warheads after separation.
*   **Defense Deficit**: The inability to intercept these weapons has raised concerns about the vulnerability of critical infrastructure.

---

## The Egypt-Turkey Strategic Pact: A Joint Drone Venture

The growing cooperation between Cairo and Ankara has changed the regional defense landscape:
*   **Joint Defense**: Egypt and Turkey established a joint venture to manufacture military drones and armored vehicles.
*   **Industrial Base**: The agreement establishes assembly plants in Egypt, reducing dependence on Western arms imports.
*   **Strategic Shift**: The partnership marks the end of a 13-year diplomatic freeze, creating a new regional bloc.

---

## Combined Maneuvers: From Bader 2026 to Raad 2

The allied nations have executed a series of large-scale military exercises:
*   **Bader 2026**: Egypt conducted tactical maneuvers near the border, simulating defensive operations in Sinai.
*   **Raad 2**: Egyptian and Pakistani units executed joint training exercises in Pakistan focusing on special operations.
*   **Strategic Routines**: Brick warned that these regular drills could be used to cover preparations for a sudden deployment.

---

## Jabal Halal Fortifications: Bunkers Bypassing GBU-57 Bombs

The Egyptian military has constructed deep underground command facilities in Sinai:
*   **Jabal Halal**: Reinforced command bunkers have been constructed at a depth of 60 meters inside Jabal Halal.
*   **GBU-57 Immunity**: The depth of the facilities protects them from U.S. GBU-57 bunker-buster bombs.
*   **Official Cover**: The government officially designated the excavations as civilian food processing sites to avoid treaty scrutiny.

---

## The Multi-State Coalition: Integrating Capital and Nuclear Power

The emerging alliance combines the resources of several regional powers:
*   **The Coalition**: The partnership links Egypt, Turkey, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia, with Sudan as a potential partner.
*   **Resource Integration**: The bloc combines Saudi financial capital, Pakistani nuclear deterrence, and Turkish manufacturing.
*   **Security Implications**: The integration of these capabilities creates a significant challenge to Israeli regional policy.

---

## Conclusion: The Structural Warnings of General Yitzhak Brick

Major General Yitzhak Brick's warnings highlight the challenges facing Israel's security concept.

By pointing to the grounding of F-15 and F-35 fighter jets, the re-organization of Hamas, and the joint Egyptian-Turkish drone venture, Brick challenges the narrative of military success. The construction of deep bunkers in Jabal Halal and the strategic exercises like Bader 2026 show that regional powers are developing significant capabilities. As Brick warns of existential risks, the emerging coalition of Egypt, Turkey, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia shows a changing security environment in the Middle East.
