# True Promise 4: The Collapse of Oil Diplomacy

Following the failure of the backchannel diplomatic initiative mediated by Moscow, the military confrontation in the Middle East has entered its most intense phase. The collapse of the proposed Trump-Putin agreement, which sought to trade diplomatic concessions in Ukraine for oil price stabilization and mediation with Iran, has removed all political constraints. Iran has responded by launching \"Operation True Promise 4\" (الوعد الصادق 4), utilizing a new generation of high-precision heavy ballistic missiles.

## The Collapse of the Trump-Putin Ceasefire Initiative

The diplomatic framework proposed by the U.S. and Russian administrations fell apart after key stakeholders in Tehran and Kyiv rejected the terms of the trade-off. The initiative sought to freeze the conflict in Ukraine while offering concessions to Russia, in exchange for Russian assistance in stabilizing global oil prices and bringing Iran to the negotiating table.

Tehran viewed the terms as an attempt to restrict its defensive options without addressing core security concerns, while Ukraine opposed freezing its borders under Russian control. The collapse of these negotiations has left both the U.S. and Iran committed to resolving their strategic differences on the battlefield.

---

## Heavy Bomber Strikes and Non-Conventional Escalation

Immediately following the failure of the diplomatic initiative, U.S. and Israeli forces launched a massive wave of airstrikes targeting Iranian military installations and civilian infrastructure. The coalition deployed its full strategic bomber fleet, including B-1B Lancer, B-2 Spirit, and next-generation B-21 Raider aircraft operating from British bases.

Simultaneously, the Israeli military deployed heavy surface-to-surface ballistic systems, including Jericho (أريحا) missiles launched from bases near Bnei Brak, east of Tel Aviv. These heavy strikes were supported by U.S. HIMARS rocket systems positioned in the Persian Gulf, aiming to sever Iran's command links.

---

## Operation True Promise 4: The Strategic Targets

Iran's response, code-named Operation True Promise 4, involved launching waves 34 and 35 of ballistic missiles and suicide drones. The operation targeted critical U.S. and Israeli military assets in the region:
*   **U.S. Bases**: Heavy missile salvos struck the U.S. Fifth Fleet headquarters at Juffair Naval Base in Bahrain and air facilities at Al Dhafra Airbase in the UAE.
*   **Northern Israel**: Targets included Ramat David Airbase, Haifa Civil Airport, and the military surveillance center in Haifa that processes spy satellite data.
*   **Infrastructure**: Suicide drones and ballistic missiles targeted Haifa's oil refineries and fuel storage facilities in response to strikes on Iranian oil assets.

---

## The "Noghtavi" Precision Doctrine: One-Ton Warheads

A key feature of Operation True Promise 4 is the deployment of "Noghtavi" (point-target/نقطوي) precision-guided missiles. This term, derived from the Persian word for "point," refers to guidance systems capable of hitting a specific target with surgical accuracy.

These missiles feature a Circular Error Probable (CEP) of less than five meters. This accuracy eliminates the need for area-saturation tactics, allowing single missiles to destroy hardened structures. During this operation, Iran deployed these precision guidance packages on warheads weighing more than one ton (1,000 kilograms), delivering high explosive yields directly onto high-value targets.

---

## Upgraded Ballistic Systems: Khorramshahr-4 and Solid-Fuel Haj Qasem

The missile salvos utilized several of Iran's most advanced ballistic systems:
*   **Khorramshahr-4 (خرمشهر 4)**: A heavy liquid-fueled missile with a range of 2,000 kilometers, carrying a guided warhead exceeding one ton.
*   **Haj Qasem (حاج قاسم)**: Iran's first solid-fuel precision ballistic missile, featuring a range of 1,400 kilometers and an upgraded, highly destructive warhead.
*   **Fattah and Kheibar**: Hypersonic and precision-guided variants (including the Fattah system operated by Hezbollah) were deployed to penetrate Israel's terminal air defense networks.
*   **Emad and Qadr**: Upgraded 2026 variants equipped with maneuverable re-entry vehicles (MaRVs) featuring aerodynamic fins, enabling them to execute evasive maneuvers during the final phase of flight.

## Countering the "No Military Power Remaining" Narrative

The scale of Operation True Promise 4 directly challenges the U.S. administration's public narrative. In a phone interview with CNBC news, Donald Trump claimed that the military campaign had effectively ended Iran's combat capabilities, stating that they had "no navy, no communications, no air force, and their missiles were scattered and destroyed."

By executing coordinated strikes across multiple countries on day 11 of the escalation, Iran demonstrated that its strategic command remains intact. The IRGC confirmed it retains full control over the Strait of Hormuz, challenging U.S. claims and warning that any attempt to escort oil tankers through the strait would be met with direct force.

---

## Conclusion: The Horizon of Unrestricted Regional Warfare

The failure of the Trump-Putin diplomatic initiative and the launch of Operation True Promise 4 marks a significant escalation in the conflict. By deploying one-ton precision warheads against U.S. and Israeli installations, Iran has shown it can sustain high-intensity operations despite heavy bombardment.

As the U.S. and Israel continue their strategic bomber campaign, the lack of political constraints increases the risk of a wider regional conflict. The reliance on advanced ballistic guidance, heavy payloads, and coordinated coalition actions suggests that both sides are prepared for a long-term confrontation, changing the security dynamics of the Middle East.
