# Iran Submarines Enter Med: US Sub Stand-off

The maritime confrontation between the U.S. and Iran has expanded into the Mediterranean Sea following the deployment of strategic Iranian submarines to the eastern basin. The deployment, confirmed by Admiral Habibollah Sayyari, Chief of Staff of the Iranian Army, has established a direct undersea stand-off with the U.S. Navy's Ohio-class submarine USS *Alaska* (SSBN-732). The development represents a shift in Iran's naval strategy, challenging NATO's basing assumptions in the region.

## Undersea Maneuvers: Iranian Submarines Enter the Mediterranean

The arrival of Iranian undersea assets in the eastern Mediterranean has changed regional security assumptions:
*   **The Deployment**: Iranian strategic submarines entered the Mediterranean basin, executing patrols off the Syrian coast.
*   **Strategic Intent**: The deployment seeks to shadow U.S. capital ships and establish a presence near coastal gas installations.
*   **Undetected Transit**: The vessels completed the transit without being intercepted by regional surveillance networks.

## Bab al-Mandeb Transit: Bypassing the Regional Maritime Blockade

The submarine transit was executed through contested chokepoints:
*   **Bab al-Mandeb**: The vessels transited the Bab al-Mandeb strait under the cover of decoy operations by local forces.
*   **Red Sea Crossing**: The submarines completed a submerged transit of the Red Sea, passing through the Suez Canal.
*   **Bypassing Blockades**: The successful transit shows that undersea platforms can bypass the shipping blockades enforced in the Gulf.

## The Ohio-Class Counterweight: USS Alaska Deploys to East Med

The U.S. has positioned its premier ballistic missile submarine to counter the undersea deployment:
*   **USS Alaska**: The Ohio-class submarine *USS Alaska* (SSBN-732) has been deployed to the eastern Mediterranean.
*   **Ballistic Payload**: The submarine carries 20 Trident II D5 nuclear missiles, serving as a strategic deterrent.
*   **Undersea Stand-off**: The presence of both nations' submarines has created a direct undersea standoff in the eastern basin.

*   **Undersea Threat**: The presence of adversary submarines near these hubs complicates the security of logistics shipping.

The deployment of Iranian submarines to the eastern Mediterranean shows the expansion of the maritime standoff.
