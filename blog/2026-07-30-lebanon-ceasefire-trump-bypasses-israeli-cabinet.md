# Trump Lebanon Ceasefire Bypasses Israeli Cabinet

In a diplomatic shift that has surprised Tel Aviv, U.S. President Donald Trump has unilaterally announced a 10-day ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon. The decision, delivered via direct phone calls while the Israeli cabinet was in session, bypasses Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's decision-making process. The announcement marks the collapse of five core conditions Netanyahu had insisted upon, shifting the dynamic of the Middle East conflict toward the Islamabad negotiation framework.

## The Unilateral Ceasefire: Trump's Surprise Announcement

The U.S. administration has forced a halt to operations to safeguard regional negotiations:
*   **The Announcement**: Trump declared the 10-day ceasefire active starting at 5:00 PM Eastern Time.
*   **Direct Channel**: The decision was communicated directly to Lebanese and Israeli leaders, bypassing diplomatic envoys.
*   **The Goal**: Establishing a temporary truce to allow the Islamabad summit to address nuclear and border disputes.

---

## Stunned Cabinet: Netanyahu Confronts a Fait Accompli

The timing of the announcement caused confusion within the Israeli government:
*   **The Meeting**: The cabinet was debating the conditions for a ceasefire when news of Trump's announcement broke.
*   **Cabinet Reaction**: Ministers expressed surprise at the unilateral decision, which bypasses the cabinet's vote.
*   **Official Acceptance**: Netanyahu accepted the decision, instructing IDF units to hold their current positions along the border.

---

## Breaking the Rule: Ending Negotiations Under Fire

The 10-day truce challenges a key tenet of Israeli military doctrine:
*   **Negotiations Under Fire**: Since October 7, Israel has maintained that it will not negotiate while under threat of rocket attacks.
*   **The Shift**: The acceptance of the ceasefire while Hezbollah rockets were striking northern towns represents a change in policy.
*   **Aide Accusations**: Right-wing coalition members accused the Prime Minister of accepting a U.S.-imposed framework.

---

## The Collapse of Condition One: Litani River Access Failed

The first failed condition relates to the positioning of Israeli ground forces:
*   **The Demand**: Israel demanded complete access to the Litani River sector to dismantle defensive tunnels.
*   **The Reality**: IDF forces were halted on the outskirts of Bint Jbeil, unable to establish a secure line to the river.
*   **Status Quo**: The ceasefire freezes troops in their current positions, leaving Hezbollah's defensive networks intact.

---

## The Collapse of Condition Two: Political Wing Ban Rejected

Netanyahu had sought to restrict Hezbollah's role in the Lebanese state:
*   **The Demand**: A ban on Hezbollah's political wing, including the closure of its bank accounts and social services.
*   **The Outcome**: Lebanese negotiators rejected the demand, maintaining that the organization's political status is a domestic matter.
*   **No Restrictions**: The ceasefire agreement contains no measures targeting the group's political representation.

---

## The Collapse of Condition Three: Paralyzing the Paratroopers

The combat capacity of elite Israeli infantry units was compromised by defensive operations:
*   **The Demand**: Unrestricted operational freedom for the IDF to execute search-and-destroy missions in southern Lebanon.
*   **The Resistance**: High casualties among the 101st Paratrooper Battalion made continuous operations difficult to sustain.
*   **Halt Order**: The truce ends active infantry operations, preventing further clearing actions.

---

## The Collapse of Condition Four: Sidelining Speaker Nabih Berri

Israeli efforts to isolate Lebanon's political leadership did not succeed:
*   **The Target**: Sidelining Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri from the ceasefire negotiation loop.
*   **The Contact**: Speaker Ghalibaf of Iran maintained direct contact with Berri to coordinate the Lebanese stance.
*   **Central Role**: Berri remained the primary intermediary, managing the flow of draft agreements to the government.

---

## The Collapse of Condition Five: Iranian Ambassador Expulsion

Israel did not succeed in breaking the diplomatic link between Beirut and Tehran:
*   **The Demand**: The immediate expulsion of the Iranian Ambassador and all IRGC personnel from Lebanon.
*   **The Status**: The Iranian Ambassador remains active in Beirut, coordinating communication with the presidency.
*   **Diplomatic Link**: The embassy continues to serve as a primary liaison office between the negotiating teams.

---

## Conclusion: Trump's White House Summons for Aoun and Netanyahu

The unilateral ceasefire imposed by Donald Trump marks the end of Israel's attempt to dictate ceasefire terms.

By bypassing the Israeli cabinet, the 10-day truce led to the collapse of Netanyahu's key demands, including Litani River control and the expulsion of the Iranian Ambassador. The resistance of paratrooper units in Bint Jbeil and the coordination between Nabih Berri and Tehran confirmed that military actions did not achieve a decisive outcome. As Trump summons regional leaders to Washington, the transition from military operations to diplomatic negotiations shows the limits of unilateral actions.
