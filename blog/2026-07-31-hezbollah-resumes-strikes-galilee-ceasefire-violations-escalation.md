# Hezbollah Resumes Strikes: 22 Operations Hit Galilee

A significant escalation has challenged the active ceasefire along the Lebanon-Israel border, with Hezbollah executing 22 coordinated military operations in a single day. The strikes, involving live-fire rocket salvos and low-altitude kamikaze drones, targeted positions across the western Galilee, wounding 21 Israeli soldiers. The operations represent a response to Israeli airstrikes in the Bekaa Valley, signaling a return to active combat.

## Ceasefire Breach: Twenty-Two Coordinated Operations

The border region experienced its highest volume of strikes since the ceasefire went into effect:
*   **The Scope**: Faction forces executed 22 separate operations targeting military positions along the frontier.
*   **Casualty Toll**: Medical teams confirmed that 21 Israeli soldiers were wounded in the attacks.
*   **The Cause**: Hezbollah stated the operations were in response to ongoing Israeli violations, including demolitions.

---

## Sirens in Galilee: Rocket Alerts Reach Western Coast

For the first time since the truce began, warning systems were activated in multiple towns:
*   **Alert Zone**: Rocket sirens sounded in Shlomi, Ya'ara, Rosh HaNikra, Liman, and Hanita.
*   **Bar'am Attack**: Direct rocket fire hit the Hanita corridor and the kibbutz of Bar'am.
*   **Civilian Safe-Rooms**: Local residents were instructed to seek shelter immediately as the sirens sounded.

---

## Metula Incident: Intact Kamikaze Drone Under Analysis

A technical inspection was launched following the recovery of a downed platform:
*   **The Discovery**: An intact kamikaze drone was found landed in an open field near Metula.
*   **Technical Probe**: IDF engineering units recovered the drone to analyze its guidance systems.
*   **Fiber-Optic Link**: The intact unit is believed to utilize the new fiber-optic tether system.

---

## Excavator Target: Drone Strikes near al-Abbad Post

Engineering machinery involved in border fortification work was targeted:
*   **The Strike**: A suicide drone struck an armored excavator operating near the al-Abbad post.
*   **The Damage**: The impact disabled the machinery, halting fortification work in the sector.
*   **Sovereign Response**: Hezbollah stated the strike was executed to prevent the creation of new border fences.

---

## The Biyadah Strikes: Suicide Drones Target Troop Clusters

Troop concentrations in forward areas were subjected to coordinate aerial attacks:
*   **Biyadah Operations**: Two kamikaze drones struck an infantry gathering in the border town of al-Biyadah.
*   **Secondary Hits**: Rocket fire targeted the same position shortly after the drone strikes.
*   **Evacuation Flights**: Helicopters were used to transport the wounded to regional medical centers.

---

## Air Defense Response: Ground-to-Air Engagements in Abassiyeh

Air defense assets attempted to intercept target platforms over the border:
*   **SAM Firing**: Faction units fired two surface-to-air missiles over the town of Abassiyeh.
*   **Targeting Recon**: The missiles targeted an Israeli reconnaissance drone operating in Lebanese airspace.
*   **Air Cover Gaps**: The engagement restricted the operations of Israeli drones in the immediate sector.

---

## Command Restructuring: The Financial Times Strategy Audit

International analysts have highlighted the organizational resilience of the defense networks:
*   **The FT Audit**: The *Financial Times* reported that Hezbollah has reconstructed its command chain.
*   **Tactical Change**: The group has transitioned from conventional infantry operations to decentralized cell tactics.
*   **Asset Preservation**: Decentralization has allowed the group to protect its missile stockpiles from airstrikes.

---

## Metula Council Response: Accusing the Military of Media Censorship

Local authorities have criticized the government's management of the border security situation:
*   **Azoulai's Accusation**: Metula Council head David Azoulai accused the military of enforcing a news blackout.
*   **Censorship Claims**: Azoulai asserted that command structures are hiding the severity of border incursions.
*   **Loss of Security**: Local leaders warned that residents will not return if the threat of drone attacks persists.

---

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Tactics of the Truce Escalation

The resumption of strikes with 22 coordinated operations highlights the challenges of enforcing the border ceasefire.

By executing rocket salvos across the Galilee and target-striking assets like the excavator at al-Abbad, Hezbollah has shown its capacity to bypass active defenses. The recovery of the intact drone near Metula confirms the use of fiber-optic guidance systems in the conflict. As David Azoulai accuses the military of censorship, the escalation shows that ceasefire agreements remain fragile in the face of asymmetric operations.
