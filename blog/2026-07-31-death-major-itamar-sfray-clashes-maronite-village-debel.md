# Paratrooper Killed in Debel Maronite Village

The ground war in southern Lebanon has expanded into previously neutral Christian Maronite sectors, leading to close-quarters clashes in the village of Debel. The fighting resulted in the death of Major (Res.) Itamar Sfray, deputy company commander in the 7008th Battalion of the 551st Paratroopers Brigade. The inclusion of Christian villages in the combat zone has created a unified political response in Beirut, uniting diverse factions against the military campaign.

## The Debel Incursion: Clashes Expand to Maronite Villages

Israeli ground maneuvers have entered sectors that had not seen active combat in previous phases:
*   **The Village**: Debel, a historic Maronite Christian village near the border, became a key combat zone.
*   **Tactical Change**: Deployed forces entered the sector to establish observation posts and staging areas.
*   **Resistance**: Local groups coordinated with mobile defense units to resist the establishment of military installations.

---

## Command Casualty: The Death of Major Itamar Sfray

A senior paratrooper officer was killed during close-range engagements inside the residential sector:
*   **The Officer**: Major (Res.) Itamar Sfray, aged 27, from Ma'ale Adumim, died from shrapnel wounds.
*   **Role**: Sfray served as the deputy company commander for the 7008th Battalion of the 551st Brigade.
*   **The Incident**: Sfray was hit during an exchange of fire with local defenders near a church courtyard.

---

## The Staging Zone Conflict: Forced Evacuation of Christian Sectors

The military's attempts to clear residential areas have generated local opposition:
*   **Forced Evacuation**: Command structures ordered the evacuation of Debel to create a secure military zone.
*   **Demolition Risk**: Local residents feared the clearing would lead to the destruction of historic Maronite structures.
*   **Active Opposition**: The refusal to leave transformed the village into a highly contested urban combat zone.

---

## National Unity Shift: Joseph Aoun and Lebanese Faction Alignment

The expansion of the conflict into Christian sectors has affected the political dynamics in Beirut:
*   **Joseph Aoun**: Lebanese Army Commander General Joseph Aoun coordinated with political leaders to demand a truce.
*   **Faction Alignment**: Maronite, Sunni, and Shiite leaders aligned on a unified national stance against the operations.
*   **Ceasefire Priority**: The government rejected calls for unilateral disarmament, prioritizing an immediate ceasefire.

---

## Church Perimeter Clashes: Close-Quarter Combat in Debel

The fighting inside Debel occurred within dense residential areas:
*   **Perimeter Combat**: Paratroopers clashed with defenders near the central Maronite church.
*   **Urban Barriers**: The stone structures of the village provided cover, limiting the effectiveness of drone surveillance.
*   **Casualty Evacuation**: Medics faced significant challenges evacuating wounded personnel due to active sniper fire.

---

## The Iskandaroun Strike: Kamikaze Drones Target Border Gatherings

Hezbollah launched coordinated strikes on logistics hubs supporting the forward units:
*   **Iskandaroun Strike**: A kamikaze drone struck an IDF vehicle gathering in the Iskandaroun sector.
*   **Tactical Timing**: The strike occurred as supply trucks were preparing to transport ammunition to the front.
*   **Logistical Disruption**: The explosion damaged two logistics trucks, halting supply movements for several hours.

---

## Naqoura Port Engagements: Drones and Rockets Strike IDF Formations

Naval and port facilities used by security forces were targeted by coordinated attacks:
*   **Naqoura Port**: A swarm of kamikaze drones struck troop positions near the port of Naqoura.
*   **Combined Fire**: The drone strikes were coordinated with heavy mortar fire and Grad rocket salvos.
*   **Defensive Gaps**: The coordinated attack bypassed coastal monitoring systems, hitting targets inside the port.

---

## Political Backlash: White House Christian Advisors Urge Truce

The targeting of Christian villages has created diplomatic challenges for the administration:
*   **US Backlash**: Christian Lebanese-American advisors in Washington urged the White House to intervene.
*   **Truce Demands**: The advisors warned that the destruction of Maronite villages would harm relations with key allies.
*   **Netanyahu Pressured**: U.S. diplomats pressured the Israeli Prime Minister to respect the neutrality of Christian towns.

---

## Conclusion: The Unified Resistance Against Territorial Expansion

The clashes in Debel and the death of Major Itamar Sfray show the consequences of expanding the combat zone into Maronite villages.

By attempting to evacuate Debel and establish military staging zones near historic churches, the IDF has created a unified national resistance. The coordination between General Joseph Aoun and various Lebanese factions shows a shift in political dynamics in Beirut. As White House advisors call for a truce, the incidents confirm that operations in Christian sectors face significant political and military obstacles.
