# IRGC Strikes Muwaffaq Salti Base: MQ-9 Downed

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has executed precision strikes targeting Muwaffaq Salti Airbase in Jordan as part of a 21-target retaliatory campaign. The operation targeted U.S. F-35 hangars and command centers at the base. In a parallel engagement, Iranian and allied forces shot down a U.S. MQ-9 Reaper drone over the Gulf, matching previous U.S. aviation losses in the region.

## The 21-Target Equation: Tehran Matches U.S. Strike Numbers

Tehran has implemented a specific numeric policy to manage its military retaliation:
*   **The Equation**: The IRGC targeted exactly 21 U.S. support and logistics sites in the region.
*   **Response Scale**: The number matches and exceeds the 20 targets claimed by U.S. strikes.
*   **Parity Signal**: The policy indicates that any U.S. strike will be met with a larger retaliatory wave.

## Striking Jordan: Muwaffaq Salti Airbase in Azraq Struck

One of the primary targets of the Iranian ballistic missile salvos was the airbase in eastern Jordan:
*   **Muwaffaq Salti**: The strategic airbase in Azraq was hit by multiple Kheibar Shekan missiles.
*   **Logistics Hub**: The base serves as a major logistics hub for U.S. and allied aviation wings.
*   **Strike Scope**: Missiles targeted maintenance bays, runways, and coordination sectors.

## Fighter Hangars Targeted: F-35 Facilities Hit at Azraq Base

The strike plan prioritized hangars hosting advanced strike aircraft:
*   **F-35 Hangars**: Reinforced hangars hosting F-35 fighter wings sustained direct hits.
