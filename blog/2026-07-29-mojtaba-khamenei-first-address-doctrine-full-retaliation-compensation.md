# Mojtaba Khamenei's Doctrine: Retaliation & Compensation

In his first official address to the nation, Mojtaba Hosseini Khamenei—the designated successor to Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei—has outlined a major shift in Iran's military and diplomatic doctrine. Moving beyond the limited containment strategies that characterized the previous 12-day war, the new leadership has established two unyielding pillars for ending the conflict: "Full Retaliation" (الانتقام الكامل) and "Full Compensation" (التعويض الكامل). This address establishes a new framework for any future ceasefire negotiations.

## Mojtaba Khamenei's First Address: Setting a New Tone

The public address by Mojtaba Khamenei represents his formal entry into the strategic decision-making process. The speech focused on defining the long-term parameters of the confrontation with the U.S. and Israel, projecting ideological and military resolve.

A notable feature of the address was the focus on the "Zionist Fitnah" (الفتنة الصهيونية) as the primary driver of the conflict. By framing U.S. President Donald Trump as "seduced" or "infatuated" (مفتون) by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's actions, Mojtaba left room for diplomatic and financial settlements with Washington, while holding Israel responsible for initiating the instability.

---

## The Doctrine of Full Retaliation: No Citizens Left Behind

The first pillar of the new doctrine is the concept of Full Retaliation. Mojtaba Khamenei declared that Iran's response will not be limited to symbolic strikes or targeting specific commanders. Instead, every Iranian citizen who falls victim to coalition strikes constitutes an independent file of retaliation.

He stated: "We will not abandon the revenge for the blood of your martyrs. The retaliation we mean does not stop at commanders; every individual citizen who falls at the hands of the enemy constitutes an independent case in the file of revenge, which remains open until it is fully resolved."

---

## The Doctrine of Full Compensation: A New Sovereign Demand

The second pillar of the doctrine is the demand for Full Compensation. This represents a significant departure from Ali Khamenei's previous approach, where financial compensation was never presented as a primary war aim.

President Masoud Pezeshkian has integrated this demand into diplomatic communications, presenting it to Russian mediators as a key condition for any ceasefire. Under this doctrine, the U.S. and its partners must pay full state-level reparations for all physical, industrial, and human damage caused by their military campaign, including the civilian casualties in Minab and the girls of Tayyebat.

---

## Enforcement Through Destruction: The Seizure of Assets

The new doctrine establishes clear enforcement mechanisms if the U.S. and Israel refuse to pay reparations. Mojtaba Khamenei warned that Iran will utilize asymmetric means to extract what it is owed.

He stated: "We will demand that the enemy pay compensation. If they refuse, we will seize their assets to the extent we deem appropriate. If that is not possible, we will systematically destroy equivalent assets of theirs." This policy directly links the destruction of Iranian infrastructure to reciprocal strikes against U.S. and Israeli logistical, financial, and maritime assets in the region.

---

## The U.S. Humanitarian Compensation Strategy and Minab

In response to the demand for reparations, the U.S. intelligence community has suggested a alternative framework. To avoid establishing a precedent of paying state-level war reparations, U.S. planners have proposed direct humanitarian payouts to victims' families, specifically targeting the families of the Minab school strike.

The Iranian leadership has rejected this proposal, viewing it as an attempt to bypass state accountability. Tehran insists that all compensation must be paid directly to the state treasury as formal reparations, ensuring that the U.S. administration accepts legal responsibility for the consequences of its campaign.

---

## Advice to Regional States Hosting U.S. Bases

Mojtaba Khamenei addressed neighboring countries hosting U.S. military installations, advising them to shut down these bases to protect their own security. However, he framed this as "advice" (نصيحة) rather than an ultimatum.

He introduced a new jurisprudential decree: if regional governments publicly condemn the U.S.-Israeli strikes and deny them political legitimacy, Iran will treat the U.S. use of their territory as "forced utilization" (استغلال قسري). Under this decree, Iran will not hold host nations responsible or target their domestic assets, reserving its strikes strictly for the U.S. military components.

---

## Conclusion: The Transition from Containment to Deterrence

The first address by Mojtaba Khamenei outlines a new strategic reality for the Middle East. By establishing full retaliation and compensation as conditions for peace, the new leadership has raised the stakes for the U.S. and Israel.

The warning that Iran will seize or destroy equivalent assets if compensation is denied suggests that the conflict will remain volatile even during ceasefire negotiations. As regional states assess their position regarding U.S. bases, the new doctrine will shape the diplomatic and military dynamics, signaling that Tehran is prepared for a long-term confrontation to protect its sovereign interests.
