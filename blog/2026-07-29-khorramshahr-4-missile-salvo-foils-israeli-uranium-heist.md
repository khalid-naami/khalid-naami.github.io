# Foiled Heist: Khorramshahr-4 Salvo Blocks Israeli Raid

A massive Iranian ballistic missile strike, code-named \"Khorramshahr-4 Night,\" has disrupted a coordinated U.S.-Israeli plan to execute a high-risk commando raid inside Iran. The strike, consisting of waves 36 and 37 of ballistic missiles, saturated regional air defense networks and directly targeted U.S. and Israeli hubs. By turning the airspace into a combat zone, the Iranian barrage foiled a top-secret mission to extract hundreds of kilograms of enriched uranium.

## The \"Khorramshahr-4 Night\": Fipping the Escalation Equation

The escalation, which took place on Laylat al-Qadr—a night of deep religious significance in the region—began as U.S. and Israeli planners prepared for their heaviest night of bombing in Iran. However, the IRGC preempted the coalition by launching a massive ballistic salvo that lasted for 90 minutes.

Iranian forces fired hundreds of heavy missiles, including the Khorramshahr-4 (خرمشهر 4), hypersonic Kheibar, and upgraded Qadr systems carrying MIRV (multiple independently targetable reentry vehicle) warheads. The heavy bombardment struck targets south of Tel Aviv, West Jerusalem, Haifa, Be'er Ya'akov, Juffair Naval Base in Bahrain, and U.S. facilities in Erbil, reversing the escalation dynamics.

---

## The Foiled Uranium Heist: Israel’s Commando Strategy

The primary objective of the U.S.-backed Israeli plan was a commando infiltration targeting Iran's uranium enrichment facilities. The goal of the raid was to extract approximately 450 kilograms of highly enriched uranium from the heart of the country.

U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth had coordinated a massive air campaign to provide fire cover for the special forces. By executing the raid, presenting the uranium to the media the next morning, and declaring a decisive victory, the coalition hoped to force an end to the war. However, the intense Iranian missile barrage blocked the entry of the transport aircraft and commando teams.

---

## The Dan Weiss Leak: Disappointment in Tel Aviv

The failure of the commando raid has caused significant frustration within Israel's defense establishment. The details of the planned infiltration were leaked in an article by Dan Weiss (Dan Senor), exposing the target list and the strategic parameters of the operation.

Israeli commanders expressed disappointment over the leak, arguing that it compromised the element of surprise. Combined with the massive Iranian missile storm that closed regional corridors, the leak made the extraction of the 450 kilograms of uranium impossible, forcing the military to abandon the plan.

---

## The IRGC Statement: Religious Symbolism and Strategic Resolve

The launch timing on Laylat al-Qadr was heavily emphasized in the IRGC's official communications. The statement released by the Guard declared: \"The God of Moses and Aaron sends down the fire of His wrath upon the Pharaohs of this age.\"

This messaging was designed to project ideological resolve, framing the missile strikes as a direct counter-offensive against the coalition's campaign. By linking the military action to historical and religious narratives, the IRGC sought to rally domestic and regional support during a critical phase of the confrontation.

---

## Tactical Upgrades: Hypersonic Kheibar and Multiple-Warhead Emad

The \"Khorramshahr-4 Night\" showcased the rapid deployment of upgraded ballistic missile systems developed by Iran since the 12-day war in 2025. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu previously claimed that the IAF had destroyed Iran's missile industry.

However, the deployment of upgraded systems challenged this claim:
*   **Khorramshahr-4**: Deployed with heavy warheads exceeding one ton, carrying cluster payloads.
*   **Emad (Aetimad)**: Upgraded with maneuverable re-entry vehicles (MaRVs) and multiple guided warheads to saturate target zones.
*   **Hypersonic Kheibar**: Used to target high-value facilities, demonstrating high terminal velocities that limit air defense reaction times.

---

## Bypassing Arrow Defenses: Sating Regional Airspace

During the 90-minute barrage, Israeli air defense networks faced significant difficulties intercepting the incoming ballistic threats. While shorter-range rocket barrages from southern Lebanon are regularly engaged by Iron Dome and David's Sling, the heavy, high-velocity warheads of the Khorramshahr-4 and Kheibar bypassed the Arrow-3 defensive umbrella.

The volume of incoming heavy warheads saturated the radar arrays, allowing several missiles to impact strategic targets. This showed that regional defenses could be overwhelmed by coordinated, high-volume ballistic salvos.

---

## Conclusion: The Limits of Preemptive Infrastructure Strikes

The failure of the U.S.-backed commando raid to extract the enriched uranium demonstrates the limits of air superiority. Despite consecutive nights of heavy strategic bomber sorties, the coalition was unable to secure the airspace or prevent the IRGC from launching coordinated counter-strikes.

As both sides assess the results of the \"Khorramshahr-4 Night,\" the strategic options for a quick conclusion to the conflict have narrowed. With the commando option blocked and energy markets highly volatile, the confrontation continues to develop into a regional war of attrition, highlighting the difficulty of achieving a decisive victory through air operations alone.
