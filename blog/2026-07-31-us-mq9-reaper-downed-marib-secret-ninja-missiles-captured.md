# US MQ-9 Downed: Secret Ninja Missiles Captured

A U.S. Air Force MQ-9 Reaper reconnaissance drone has been shot down over the Marib province in northeast Yemen. According to military assessments, the downed platform was carrying classified AGM-114R9X Hellfire missiles, commonly known as the "Ninja" or "Flying Ginsu" missile. The retrieval of intact components and guidance systems from the crash site represents a significant technology transfer risk to regional and international actors.

## The Marib Incident: U.S. MQ-9 Reaper Downed in Yemen

The surveillance platform was intercepted during a patrol mission over contested sectors:
*   **The Intercept**: An MQ-9 Reaper drone was shot down over the northern Marib province.
*   **Wreckage Recovery**: Local ground forces secured the crash site immediately after the impact.

## AGM-114R9X Ninja Missiles: The Secret Kinetic Warhead Payload

The downed drone was armed with specialized precision-guided munitions:
*   **The Munition**: The drone carried AGM-114R9X missiles, designed for low-collateral targeted strikes.
*   **Kinetic Design**: The missile does not carry explosive warheads, relying on six deployable steel blades.
*   **High Secrecy**: The guidance and deployment mechanisms of the R9X are classified close-hold technologies.
