# Qaani Responds: Accustom Yourselves to the New Order

IRGC Quds Force Commander Brigadier General Esmail Qaani has issued a public message addressing the geopolitical plans proposed by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. In a statement published by *Tasnim News Agency*, Qaani declared that U.S. and Israeli attempts to bypass the Strait of Hormuz through regional pipeline corridors have failed. Addressing Netanyahu's statements at the *Maariv* Economic Conference, Qaani warned Western planners to "accustom yourselves to the new regional order" (عودوا أنفسكم على النظام الجديد للمنطقة) led by the axis of resistance.

## The Qaani Message: Accustom Yourselves to the New Order

Esmail Qaani's public appearance and statement, carried by *Tasnim*, marks a direct response to Israeli economic planning. The commander targeted Netanyahu's vision of a reshaped Middle East.

Qaani declared that the strategic actions of Hezbollah in the north and Ansar Allah in the south have neutralized Israel's promises of regional stability to U.S. investors. He warned that the era of Western transit hegemony is concluding, and that the regional security framework will be determined by local resistance forces.

---

## Shattering the Execution Myth: An Intelligence Deficit

The public broadcast of Qaani's message has had an immediate impact on the Israeli defense establishment.

For weeks, Israeli and Western media outlets had circulated reports claiming that Qaani had been executed by the Iranian regime due to alleged ties to Western intelligence. The broadcast of his video message has disproved these claims, exposing an intelligence deficit within the coalition's psychological operations wing.

## The Battle of Corridors: IMEC/I2U2 vs. Belt and Road

The core of the conflict is a clash between competing infrastructure corridors. The U.S. and Israel, along with the UAE and India, are attempting to implement:
*   **The I2U2/IMEC Corridor**: A transport network designed to link India to Europe via the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, and Israel.
*   **The Objective**: Bypassing the Strait of Hormuz and the Suez Canal, neutralizing Iran's geographic influence.
*   **The Conflict**: The IRGC views the campaign against Gaza and Lebanon as a territorial clearing operation to secure this corridor, which is designed to block China's Belt and Road path in West Asia.

---

## The Tapline Restructuring: Bypassing Sidon for Baniyas

The restructuring plan includes rebuilding historical oil transport lines. Regional media outlets have revealed draft plans to alter the Trans-Arabian Pipeline (Tapline / تي أي بي):
*   **The Route**: A 1,214-kilometer pipeline designed to transport 4 million barrels of oil daily from Saudi Arabia's Qaisumah field.
*   **The Alteration**: The new draft redirects the terminal from Sidon in Lebanon to Baniyas or Latakia in Syria.
*   **The Justification**: The shift is designed to bypass Hezbollah's territorial control in southern Lebanon, allowing the coalition to export Saudi crude directly to the Mediterranean under the oversight of Western-aligned entities.

## The Al Jazeera Controversy: Feisal al-Qassem's Tweet

The Israeli newspaper *Maariv* celebrated a social media post by prominent Al Jazeera journalist Feisal al-Qassem, who has over 6 million followers:
*   **The Post**: Al-Qassem claimed that Iran's blockade of the Strait of Hormuz was driving the world to find alternative routes through Israeli ports, playing directly into Netanyahu's hands.
*   **The Backlash**: Iranian and regional analysts criticized the post, arguing that al-Qassem was validating the U.S.-Israeli plan to marginalize regional channels under the guise of reporting on Iranian aggression.

---

## Netanyahu's Mediterranean Vision: Shifting Oil to Haifa

Netanyahu and his advisors have confirmed that the war's ultimate economic objective is to redirect regional energy flows:
*   **Netanyahu's Statement**: The Prime Minister stated that \"ideas to transport oil from the Gulf to the Mediterranean via Israel are actively being coordinated.\"
*   **The Economic Vision**: Outlined by economic director Brig. Gen. Omid Shachid, the plan aims to make Haifa the primary energy terminal for European imports, bypassing Hormuz entirely.

---

## Yemen's Strategic Deterrence: Neutralizing the Gerald Ford

In his message, Qaani praised the strategic performance of Yemen's Ansar Allah, highlighting their role in the naval theater:
*   **The USS Gerald R. Ford**: Qaani credited Yemeni missile units with forcing the withdrawal of the aircraft carrier USS Gerald R. Ford from the Red Sea.
*   **Red Sea Neutralization**: By targeting U.S. naval assets, the Houthis prevented the coalition from utilizing the Red Sea as a secure launching pad for strikes against western Iran.
*   **Strategic Wisdom**: He praised the Yemeni leadership for their timing, which coordinated with the broader axis operations.

---

## The Double-Strait Squeeze: Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab

The resistance axis has warned that any attempt to bypass the Strait of Hormuz will trigger a wider maritime blockade.

If the U.S. and Israel attempt to forcefully open Hormuz by military means, the Quds Force will coordinate a simultaneous closure of Bab al-Mandab. By restricting shipping in both the Persian Gulf and the Red Sea, the axis can cut off 30% of global oil transit, driving prices to $200 per barrel and forcing a global economic halt.

---

## Conclusion: The Fall of the Western Transit Hegemony

Brigadier General Esmail Qaani's response to Netanyahu's corridor plans confirms that the battle for the Middle East is fundamentally an infrastructure war.

While the U.S., Israel, and the UAE attempt to construct the IMEC corridor to bypass the Strait of Hormuz, the axis of resistance has established a double blockade across the region's primary maritime corridors. The failure to secure the Red Sea from Yemeni missiles and the ongoing strikes on Haifa's petrochemical terminals prove that alternative corridors cannot operate under active fire. The new regional order announced by Qaani will be determined not by Western pipelines, but by local deterrent capabilities that refuse to accept unilateral transit control.
