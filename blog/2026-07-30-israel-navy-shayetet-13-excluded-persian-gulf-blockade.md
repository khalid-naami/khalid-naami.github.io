# Israel Navy Shayetet 13 Excluded from Gulf Blockade

To prevent a total collapse of the maritime talks, the U.S. administration has ordered the exclusion of the Israeli Navy, including its elite Shayetet 13 commando unit, from operations in the Persian Gulf. The decision is a concession to Iran, which established a strict red line refusing any agreement if Israeli forces participate in the regional blockade. The pushback of Israeli naval assets to the Eastern Mediterranean highlights the diplomatic compromises required to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

## Strategic Sacrifice: Trump Excludes Israel from the Gulf

The U.S. administration has prioritized securing an agreement over maintaining a joint front:
*   **The Decision**: Trump requested the complete withdrawal of Israeli naval liaison officers and special units from Gulf operations.
*   **Bypassing Allies**: The move was made without consulting Tel Aviv, representing a unilateral shift in U.S. deployment policy.
*   **The Goal**: Removing any Israeli presence from the Gulf to allow direct nuclear and maritime negotiations to proceed.

---

## Iranian Red Line: No Agreement with Israeli Naval Presence

Tehran has maintained a clear position regarding the presence of Israeli forces in its proximity:
*   **The Red Line**: Iran stated that any direct or indirect Israeli participation in the blockade would end the truce talks.
*   **No Joint Operations**: Iranian negotiators declared that they would not sign any agreement that permits joint U.S.-Israeli naval patrols.
*   **The Leverage**: The threat to close the Strait of Hormuz provided Iran with the leverage to demand the exclusion of Israeli forces.

---

## Shayetet 13 Liaison Officers: Bahrain Base Casualties

The secret cooperation between U.S. and Israeli naval command centers has faced challenges:
*   **Bahrain Presence**: Special operators from Israel's Shayetet 13 were stationed at the U.S. Fifth Fleet base in Bahrain.
*   **The Strike**: Two Israeli liaison officers were killed in a recent Iranian missile strike on the logistical base.
*   **Security Vulnerability**: The casualties highlighted the risk of maintaining joint command centers within range of regional missile networks.

---

## Pushback to the Mediterranean: Restricting the 13th Flotilla

The operational area of the Israeli Navy has been restricted to Western sectors:
*   **Force Re-deployment**: Israeli submarines and commandos have been ordered to return to the Mediterranean.
*   **No Gulf Transit**: Israeli naval units are prohibited from crossing the Suez Canal to participate in Gulf operations.
*   **Defensive Focus**: The re-deployment focuses Israeli naval capacity on protecting domestic gas platforms and coastal infrastructure.

---

## Somaliland Logistical Detour: Bypassing Vulnerable Gulf Hubs

The U.S. Navy is establishing alternative logistical hubs to reduce its reliance on regional bases:
*   **Somaliland Hub**: The U.S. is developing port facilities in Berbera, Somaliland, as a primary support base.
*   **Vulnerability Reductions**: Bypassing ports in Bahrain and the UAE reduces the exposure of supply lines to regional missile networks.
*   **Indian Ocean Support**: The new hub allows the U.S. to support operations in the Arabian Sea without entering the Persian Gulf.

---

## Non-Traditional Blockade: Esmaeil Baghaei's Genocide Warning

Iran has challenged the legal status of the U.S. shipping restrictions:
*   **Economic Genocide**: Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei described the blockade as "economic warfare" and "genocide."
*   **Unilateral Action**: Baghaei stated that restrictions on food and fuel imports violate international law.
*   **Economic Resistance**: Iran is preparing legal and diplomatic cases to challenge the blockade at the UN.

---

## The Failure of IMMSC: Allied Reluctance to Join Blockade

The U.S.-led maritime coalition has struggled to secure international participation:
*   **Allied Reluctance**: Traditional partners, including the UK and European states, have declined to deploy warships to the blockade.
*   **Coalition Limits**: The International Maritime Security Construct (IMSC) remains dependent on U.S. hulls for enforcement operations.
*   **Political Isolation**: The lack of allied support has left the U.S. Navy to manage the blockade without a broad international mandate.

---

## UAE-Socotra Maritime Base: Sidelining Secret Logistics

The U.S. has adjusted its cooperation with regional logistics networks:
*   **Socotra Base**: The joint UAE-Israeli intelligence post on Socotra Island has been sidelined.
*   **Sourcing Shift**: Logistical operations have been moved to U.S.-only facilities to avoid regional political complications.
*   **Reducing Exposure**: The shift seeks to prevent retaliatory strikes on regional hubs that host Israeli intelligence personnel.

---

## Conclusion: The Diplomatic Price of the Hormuz Negotiations

The exclusion of Israel's Shayetet 13 from the Persian Gulf blockade highlights the diplomatic price of the Hormuz negotiations.

To prevent a breakdown of the truce talks, Donald Trump has accepted Iran's red line, ordering Israeli special units to return to the Mediterranean. The casualties among liaison officers in Bahrain and the shift to alternative bases in Somaliland show how regional missile networks have influenced naval planning. As the U.S. Navy struggles to enforce shipping restrictions without European support, the exclusion of Israeli forces confirms that regional control remains a key factor in the Persian Gulf.
