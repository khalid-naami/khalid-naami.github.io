# Muwaffaq Salti Strike: Blinding U.S. Base

Iran's IRGC has launched a precision salvo of ten ballistic missiles targeting Jordan's Muwaffaq Salti Air Base (Al-Azraq). Jordan's defense forces reported the interception of eight missiles, leaving two direct hits on command structures. The strike targets the primary regional hub utilized by U.S. CENTCOM to conduct independent air operations against Iran, bypassing coordinates managed alongside Israel.

## The Missile Salvo: Ten Ballistic Launches Target Muwaffaq Salti

The IRGC Aerospace Force targeted Jordan's main military airbase with a concentrated launch:
*   **The Salvo**: Ten medium-range ballistic missiles were fired directly at the base.
*   **Target Selection**: The facility was selected due to its role in hosting U.S. combat wings.
*   **Tactical Focus**: The strike aimed to degrade command coordination zones at the facility.

## Interception Dynamics: The Eight-to-Ten Jordan Defense Ratio

Jordanian air defense units monitored and engaged the incoming ballistic trajectory:
*   **Interceptions**: Official statements confirmed that air defense systems intercepted eight missiles.
*   **Direct Hits**: Two missiles bypassed the local screen, striking target sectors within the base.
*   **Success Rate**: The 20% penetration rate marks a shift from the complete interceptions reported previously.

Muwaffaq Salti Air Base serves as the primary outpost for unilateral U.S. operations:

*   **F-35 Deployment**: The facility hosts U.S. Air Force F-35 stealth fighter detachments.
*   **Levant Command**: The station coordinates electronic and tactical support across Syria and Iraq.
