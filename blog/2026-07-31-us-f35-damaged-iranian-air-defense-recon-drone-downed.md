# US F-35 Damaged: Iran Unified Air Defense Hit

Iran's Joint Air Defense Headquarters has announced the intercept of two U.S. aerial platforms over the Gulf. According to military reports, a U.S. reconnaissance drone was shot down in southwest Iran, while a U.S. F-35 stealth fighter under registration 13-5065 suffered damage from air defense fire. The damaged fighter executed an emergency landing at a base in the United Arab Emirates, demonstrating the capabilities of Iran's unified air defense network.
