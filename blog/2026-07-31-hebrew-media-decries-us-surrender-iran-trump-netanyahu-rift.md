# Israel Decries US Surrender: Trump-Bibi Rift

Israeli Channel 12 has described the leaked draft agreement between the U.S. and Iran as a "complete U.S. surrender." The reports highlight a growing political rift between Donald Trump and Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. Trump has reportedly declined ten consecutive requests for a direct phone call from Netanyahu, choosing instead to manage relations through aides to prevent interference in the negotiation process.

## Strategic Victory: Tehran Celebrates the Draft Treaty Terms

The leaked terms have been presented by Iranian media as a diplomatic victory:
*   **The Narrative**: Tehran has highlighted that the draft does not require the removal of enrichment assets.
*   **Sovereignty Acknowledged**: The agreement confirms Iran's primary role in managing the Strait of Hormuz.
*   **No Disarmament**: The treaty excludes U.S. demands regarding the dismantling of missile forces.

---

## Communication Blackout: Donald Trump Ignores Netanyahu's Requests

A diplomatic freeze has emerged between the U.S. President and the Israeli Prime Minister:
*   **Ten Requests**: Netanyahu reportedly requested ten separate phone calls with Trump over the past two weeks.
*   **No Direct Calls**: Trump has declined to take the calls, instructing aides to handle communications.
*   **Negotiation Protection**: The blackout is intended to prevent Netanyahu from lobbying against the draft agreement.

---

## Shifting Alliances: Trump Praises Gulf States' Financial Support

The U.S. administration has publically changed its emphasis regarding regional partners:
*   **Gulf Support**: Trump has praised Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, and Kuwait for their financial backing.
*   **Israel Omitted**: Recent White House statements have avoided highlighting Israel's role in the security architecture.
*   **High Price**: Trump noted that Gulf states have paid a higher price than Israel in supporting security operations.

---

## The Treaty Provisions: Permanent Iranian Sovereignty Over Hormuz

The draft formalizes Iran's regulatory control over the vital shipping lanes:
*   **Sovereignty Over Hormuz**: The treaty recognizes Iran's authority to manage transit in the Strait.
*   **1982 Law of the Sea**: Navigation rules will conform to the UN Convention on the Law of the Sea.
*   **Unilateral Right**: Tehran retains the right to enforce security measures during national emergencies.

---

## Reopening Channels: Resuming Pre-War Maritime Shipping Volumes

The immediate objective of the maritime agreement is the normalization of trade:
*   **Resuming Shipping**: The draft calls for returning commercial shipping numbers to pre-war levels.
*   **Fee Suspension**: Iran will temporarily suspend transit fees during the transition period.
*   **Security Guarantees**: Undersea mines will be cleared by joint teams to ensure safe transit.

---

## The Abraham Accords Collapse: IMEC Economic Corridor Deleted

The maritime terms have ended key regional economic integration projects:
*   **Abraham Accords**: The "Abrahamic economic integration" projects have been suspended.
*   **IMEC Deletion**: The India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) has been cancelled.
*   **Trade Rerouting**: The cancellation blocks planned trade links between India and Europe via the Gulf.

---

## Shipping Diversion: Israeli Merchant Vessels Forced Around Africa

Israeli commerce faces significant logistical challenges due to the maritime restrictions:
*   **Hormuz Ban**: Israeli-flagged and owned vessels are barred from entering the Persian Gulf.
*   **Africa Route**: Merchant ships must route around the Cape of Good Hope to reach Asian ports.
*   **Logistical Cost**: The diversion adds weeks to transit times and increases freight costs.

---

## Sabotaging the Deal: Netanyahu's Media Campaign Targeting Trump's Pride

Tel Aviv has launched public relations campaigns to disrupt the signing of the treaty:
*   **Surrender Narrative**: Netanyahu's cabinet has funded international media campaigns describing the deal as a surrender.
*   **Targeting Pride**: The campaign is designed to appeal to Trump's pride, encouraging him to reject the draft.
*   **Call for Rejection**: The media pushes warn that the agreement will weaken U.S. influence in the region.

---

## Conclusion: The New Diplomatic Boundaries of the Gulf Region

The Hebrew media's reaction to the draft U.S.-Iran agreement highlights the changes in regional security alignments.

By codifying Iran's sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz and closing Gulf shipping lanes to Israel, the draft treaty has disrupted the Abraham Accords and cancelled the IMEC corridor. Donald Trump's communication blackout with Netanyahu confirms the shift in White House priorities toward Gulf partnerships. As Israel launches media campaigns to disrupt the treaty, these developments confirm that the regional balance of power is transition toward new diplomatic boundaries.
