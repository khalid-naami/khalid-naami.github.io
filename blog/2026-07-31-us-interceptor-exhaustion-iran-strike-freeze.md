# US Interceptor Exhaustion: Iran Strike Freeze

The United States has frozen planned airstrikes against Iran. The decision, taken after high-level meetings at the White House, stems from warnings by the Joint Chiefs of Staff regarding a critical depletion of Patriot and other air defense interceptors across CENTCOM's area of responsibility. The freeze has left Israeli commanders in a state of strategic ambiguity as Iran restores underground missile infrastructure.
