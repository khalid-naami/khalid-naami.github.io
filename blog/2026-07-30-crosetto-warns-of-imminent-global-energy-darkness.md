# Crosetto Warns: Imminent Global Energy Darkness

Italian Defense Minister Guido Crosetto has issued a stark warning regarding the trajectory of the Middle East conflict. In a interview with the Italian newspaper *La Repubblica*, Crosetto revealed that classified joint intelligence indicates the war is approaching a phase that will irreversibly damage the global economy and plunge Europe into a severe energy crisis. The minister confirmed that U.S. plans to target Iranian oil infrastructure and Iran's prepared response against Gulf energy assets have created a scenario that could leave Europe without power by late April.

## The Crosetto Warning: A Tragedy for Global Energy

Guido Crosetto's statements to *La Repubblica* highlight the concern within European defense ministries. The minister described the coming weeks as a period that will redefine the global geopolitical landscape.

Crosetto warned that the escalation is no longer a localized military exchange between Israel and Iran. Instead, the conflict is transitioning into a structural economic war designed to reorder the global energy distribution network, with Europe positioned to bear the immediate domestic consequences.

---

## The Sleepless Nights: Access to Allied Intelligence

The Italian defense minister admitted that his warnings are based on direct access to classified allied operational plans:
*   **Sleepless Nights**: Crosetto stated: "I know things that keep me awake at night. We are forced to evaluate scenarios that are deeply concerning."
*   **Irreversible Change**: The intelligence indicates that the U.S. command has finalized its target lists for Iran's energy sector, with operations scheduled to begin before the end of April.
*   **The Threat Profile**: The intelligence details Iran's counter-strike coordinates, which focus on the total neutralization of oil extraction and refining infrastructure across the western shore of the Gulf.

---

## The Bitter Geopolitical Truth: Europe's Security Deficit

Crosetto utilized the interview to address Europe's strategic dependence on the United States:
*   **The Clay Pot**: The minister labeled Europe as the "clay pot" (القدر الفخاري للعالم) of global security, fragile and unable to defend its interests independently.
*   **Meloni's Policy**: Despite Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's alignment with Donald Trump, Crosetto asserted: "Trump walks his own path without listening to anyone, but Rome will walk its own path."
*   **Decade of Dependence**: He acknowledged that without U.S. military power, Europe would remain incapable of defending its territory for at least another decade, forcing it to accept Washington's escalatory policies.

---

## Trump's Strategic Game: The Hunt for Iranian Oil

The core driver of the impending escalation is President Trump's determination to control Iran's oil reserves. As Trump reportedly told the *Financial Times*, the war is a struggle for the physical control of the region's energy resources.

The U.S. objective is to place Iran's oil fields under direct Western administration. By doing so, Washington would secure a monopoly over the global oil supply, allowing it to dictate terms to international markets and eliminate the margin of independence previously enjoyed by producer states.

---

## The Kharg Blockade: Choking China and Subjugating Europe

The U.S. plan relies on a maritime and aerial blockade of Kharg Island, Iran's primary oil export hub:
*   **Choking China**: By halting Iranian oil exports, the U.S. aims to restrict the raw energy supply that fuels China's industrial sector.
*   **Subjugating Europe**: The blockade will force European states to purchase U.S.-sourced liquefied natural gas (LNG) and shale oil at premium prices, consolidating Washington's economic authority over its allies.
*   **Unilateral Hegemony**: For Trump, the potential destruction of Gulf oil assets is a calculated risk; the ultimate prize is another 60 years of unilateral U.S. hegemony over the global financial system.

---

## The Symmetrical Oil Threat: Burning the Gulf Infrastructure

The IRGC has warned that it will meet any attack on its energy sector with a scorched-earth campaign across the Arabian Peninsula.

If U.S. forces strike Kharg Island or attempt to blockade Iranian terminals, Iran's missile forces will target every major oil field, refinery, and pipeline in the UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, and Iraq. The objective is to ensure that if Iran cannot export oil, no other nation in the region will be permitted to do so. This symmetrical threat holds the daily energy supply of the Western hemisphere hostage.

---

## The Desalination Strike: The Humanitarian Target in Kuwait

A key element of Iran's retaliatory doctrine is targeting the critical utilities that sustain life in the Gulf:
*   **The Kuwait Precedent**: Recent drone strikes targeting power grids and water desalination plants in Kuwait demonstrated this capability.
*   **Humanitarian Pressure**: By targeting desalination facilities, the IRGC can disrupt the freshwater supply to major Gulf cities within 48 hours.
*   **Symmetrical Pain**: The strategy targets the survival infrastructure of U.S. allies, forcing them to pressure Washington to halt its offensive operations.

---

## Iran's \"Nothing to Lose\" Stance: Shattering the Sanctions

Chinese state media, writing in the *Global Times*, noted that Iran is entering this phase of the war with a distinct psychological advantage:
*   **Decades of Sanctions**: Having lived under a Western economic blockade for 47 years, Iran has already adjusted to isolation.
*   **Strategic Resolve**: The leadership views the current conflict as the final opportunity to break the sanction regime permanently through military resistance.
*   **Multipolar Shift**: Tehran is willing to accept temporary domestic damage to secure a geopolitical shift that will end U.S. financial dominance in West Asia.

---

## Conclusion: The End of U.S. Unilateral Hegemony

Defense Minister Crosetto's warnings confirm that the Middle East is on the verge of an energy crisis that will reshape the global balance of power.

If the Trump administration executes its plan to seize Iranian oil assets, the resulting Iranian counter-strikes will burn the energy infrastructure of the Gulf, plunging Europe into a deep economic depression by the end of April. While the U.S. may deploy its advanced military and AI capabilities to secure its objectives, the physical destruction of the Gulf's oil and water networks will expose the limits of Western power. The coming weeks will decide whether the U.S. can preserve its unilateral hegemony or if the collapse of the Gulf energy corridor will force a transition to a multipolar world.
