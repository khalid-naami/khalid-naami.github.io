# 60-Day War Powers: Resurrecting MQ-1s

U.S. President Donald Trump has officially notified Congress under the War Powers Resolution that U.S. forces entered active combat on July 7, initiating a 60-day window to target Iranian facilities. The deployment comes as high losses of MQ-9 Reaper drones have forced the Pentagon to resurrect retired MQ-1 Predator drones from boneyards. Concurrently, an Arleigh Burke-class destroyer and two UAE commercial ships were hit in a coordinated strike in the Gulf.

The U.S. administration has formalized the legal framework for the ongoing operations:

*   **Reactivation**: The airframes were fitted with legacy tracking systems for immediate transit.
