# Hezbollah Cruise Missiles & Israel Shekel Crisis

Following the collapse of the temporary ceasefire, Hezbollah has deployed its specialized naval assets, releasing footage of two anti-ship cruise missiles targeted at an Israeli warship. The naval deployment coincided with heavy engagements in southern Lebanon, including the downing of IDF personnel from the 13th Golani Battalion. In Tel Aviv, the economic weight of the prolonged campaign has triggered a severe financial crisis, sending the Israeli Shekel to its lowest level against the U.S. Dollar in thirty years.

## Anti-Ship Cruise Missiles: Hezbollah's Maritime Deterrence

Hezbollah's naval command has introduced high-precision anti-ship systems to the active theater:
*   **The Cruise Release**: Hezbollah published footage showing two advanced anti-ship cruise missiles locked onto an Israeli naval vessel.
*   **Active Deterrence**: The operation represents the first public deployment of Hezbollah's maritime strike capability in this round.
*   **Naval Risk**: The deployment limits Israel's freedom of maneuver along the Lebanese coast, threatening offshore gas and naval patrols.

---

## Golani Battalion Losses: The Reality of Southern Lebanon

Ground operations in southern Lebanon continue to inflict regular casualties on elite IDF units:
*   **Golani Deaths**: A soldier from the 13th Golani Battalion was confirmed killed during close-quarters combat in the border sector.
*   **Nahariya Strikes**: Over 50 rockets were launched targeting military assembly points in Nahariya and surrounding northern settlements.
*   **Operational Contrast**: The casualties occurred immediately after Tel Aviv claimed that its campaign, Operation "Eternal Darkness," had achieved all goals.

---

## Separating the Fronts: Netanyahu's Diplomatic Wedge

Israeli planners are attempting to isolate the Lebanese theater from the broader regional peace negotiations:
*   **Diplomatic Wedge**: Netanyahu is pushing to decouple the Lebanese border negotiations from the 10-point Islamabad channel.
*   **Isolating Hezbollah**: The objective is to negotiate a separate bilateral agreement with Beirut, stripping Hezbollah of Iranian diplomatic cover.
*   **The Response**: The unified resistance axis has rejected this division, demanding a comprehensive ceasefire on all fronts.

---

## Sidelining Dermer: Routing Talks Through Washington

The diplomatic channels between Tel Aviv and Washington have undergone significant restructuring:
*   **Dermer Sidelined**: Ron Dermer was sidelined by U.S. officials alongside Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff.
*   **Official Channels**: The State Department insisted on routing negotiations through the official Israeli ambassador in Washington.
*   **Policy Shift**: The shift reduces the influence of private backchannels, prioritizing institutional coordination over personal alliances.

---

## The Beirut Proposal: Karam to Lead Lebanese Team

Lebanon's leadership has asserted its authority over the negotiation process to prevent foreign interference:
*   **Lebanese Sovereignty**: President Aoun and Prime Minister Mikati rejected proposals for external actors to negotiate on Lebanon's behalf.
*   **Ambassador Karam**: Former Ambassador Simon Karam was proposed to lead the Lebanese negotiating delegation.
*   **Ceasefire Committee**: Karam's selection is based on his history in international ceasefire monitoring teams, ensuring a professional diplomatic team.

---

## Economic Crisis: The Shekel Slumps to 30-Year Low

The financial cost of the two-front war has severely impacted Israel's domestic economy:
*   **Currency Drop**: Following Netanyahu's declaration of continued operations, the Shekel dropped to 3.352 against the U.S. Dollar.
*   **30-Year Low**: This represents the lowest valuation of the Israeli currency in three decades, reflecting investor anxiety.
*   **Market Pressure**: The currency slump is driven by rising defense expenditures and the suspension of economic activity in the north.

---

## Reconstruction Demands: Northern Israel Under Pressure

The displacement of residents from northern settlements is creating domestic political instability:
*   **Economic Halt**: Manufacturing, agriculture, and tourism in Galilee and northern sectors remain entirely suspended.
*   **Rebuilding Cost**: Local councils are demanding immediate state funding to reconstruct infrastructure damaged by rocket strikes.
*   **Internal Push**: The business community is pressuring Netanyahu to accept a diplomatic solution to avoid permanent economic contraction.

---

## The Human Shield Narrative: US Intel Rejects Claims

U.S. intelligence agencies have challenged Israel's justification for strikes on residential sectors:
*   **The Narrative**: Tel Aviv has defended the bombardment of residential buildings by claiming Hezbollah uses civilians as human shields.
*   **Intel Assessment**: U.S. analysts found no evidence of active military hardware or personnel inside multiple targeted residential buildings.
*   **International Criticism**: The findings have increased diplomatic pressure from European allies, including France, Germany, and Italy.

---

## Conclusion: The Asymmetric Standoff on the Border

Hezbollah's deployment of anti-ship cruise missiles and the ongoing casualties of the Golani Battalion highlight the limits of Israel's military campaign.

Netanyahu's attempts to separate the Lebanese front from the Islamabad framework have been blocked by the unified resistance axis. The economic impact of the war, highlighted by the Shekel's drop to a 30-year low, is creating significant domestic pressure to end the conflict. With U.S. intelligence questioning the military utility of strikes on civilian infrastructure and Lebanon asserting its sovereign voice in negotiations, Tel Aviv faces a choice between a costly war of attrition and a comprehensive regional ceasefire.
