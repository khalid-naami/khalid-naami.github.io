# Mojtaba Vow: Trump's Signature is Worthless

Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei has issued a formal written declaration suspending all Iranian commitments under the recently signed U.S.-Iran memorandum of understanding. The letter, reported by Axios correspondent Barak Ravid on Israel's Channel 12, asserts that "Trump's signature is worthless" due to U.S. air strikes on Gulf targets. Mojtaba warned that if strikes do not halt within 48 hours, Iran will transition to asymmetric strikes on Gulf energy centers.
