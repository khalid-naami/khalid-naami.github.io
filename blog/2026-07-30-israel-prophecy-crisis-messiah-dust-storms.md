The Ashkenazi Chief Rabbi has publicly rebuked the Sephardi community for their failed prophecies. These theological fractures, combined with the military reality on the ground, have produced what Hebrew media calls a "collective psychological crisis" for Israel.

## The Dark Sky: Dust Storms Engulf Israel
