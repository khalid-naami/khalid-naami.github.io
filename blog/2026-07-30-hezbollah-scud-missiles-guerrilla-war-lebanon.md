# Scud-B From Lebanon: Guerrilla War Shift

Evidence has emerged that Hezbollah has launched a Scud-B ballistic missile from Lebanese territory—the first time such a weapon has been fired from Lebanon. The missile struck the Palmachim air base, marking a dramatic escalation in Hezbollah's operational capabilities. Intelligence analysts are now asking whether Hezbollah acquired parts of Syria's ballistic missile arsenal during the transitional period following the fall of Assad. On the ground, the IDF faces a fierce guerrilla war that has destroyed over 30 tanks and killed 11 soldiers since March 2026.

## The Scud Question: Syrian Arsenal Transfer

The detection of a Scud-B missile trail on camera has raised urgent intelligence questions:
*   **The Evidence**: Cameras captured the characteristic exhaust trail of a Scud-B missile launched from Lebanese territory.
*   **The Transfer**: Intelligence analysts suspect that Hezbollah acquired stockpiles from Syria's ballistic missile arsenal during the chaotic transitional period after Assad's fall.
*   **The Scale**: The transferred material is estimated to equal 18 months of the previous Iran-Syria-Lebanon logistics pipeline, giving Hezbollah a significant independent strike capability.

---

## Palmachim Struck: First Scud Hit From Lebanon

The Scud-B missile struck the Palmachim air base south of Tel Aviv:
*   **The Target**: Palmachim serves as a primary Israeli Air Force base and missile testing facility.
*   **The Impact**: Satellite imagery confirmed the Scud-B impact—the first time such a weapon has reached an Israeli military installation from Lebanon.
*   **The Implication**: If Hezbollah possesses additional Scud variants (Scud-D or modernized versions), the entire Israeli interior is within range from Lebanese launch positions.

---

## Guerrilla Transition: From Semi-Regular to Asymmetric

The fighting in South Lebanon has undergone a fundamental tactical transformation:
*   **The Shift**: Hezbollah has transitioned from semi-regular army operations to a full guerrilla warfare doctrine.
*   **Small Group Tactics**: Fighters operate in small, decentralized cells that strike IDF concentrations with precision anti-tank weapons before dispersing.
*   **Operational Tempo**: Over 48 operations were conducted in a single 24-hour period, maintaining constant pressure on Israeli forces across the entire front.

---

## Anti-Armor Dominance: 30 Tanks Destroyed

The most devastating element of Hezbollah's guerrilla campaign has been its anti-armor warfare:
*   **The Toll**: Over 30 Israeli tanks and armored vehicles have been destroyed since the escalation began.
*   **Record Reliance**: Hezbollah fighters have deployed anti-tank guided missiles (ATGMs) at an unprecedented rate, targeting every armored movement.
*   **The Method**: Targeting IDF concentrations in towns like Anata (Sidr area), striking soldiers and vehicles simultaneously with rocket salvos.

---

## Northern Settlements: Uninhabitable Zones

Hezbollah's rocket campaign has rendered large sections of northern Israel uninhabitable:
*   **Kiryat Shmona**: Under continuous rocket salvos designed to permanently evacuate the civilian population.
*   **Beyond the Blue Line**: Settlements like Malkiyeh, Yir'on, and Even Menachem have been struck with a mix of rocket salvos and suicide drones, calibrated by target type.
*   **Haifa Periphery**: Kiryat Ata, east of Haifa, has been struck, extending the threat zone deep into Israel's third-largest city.
*   **Northern Command**: The IDF's Northern Command headquarters at Dadu base, north of Safed, was targeted directly.

---

## SAM Against Helicopters: A New Capability

A significant tactical development occurred on Thursday, April 2, 2026:
*   **The Incident**: At 10:00 AM, a surface-to-air missile was fired at an IDF helicopter over the border town of Ramia.
*   **The Message**: The deployment of SAM systems against rotary-wing aircraft demonstrates that Hezbollah's air denial capabilities have expanded beyond drones and rockets.
*   **The Impact**: Israeli helicopters—critical for medical evacuation and close air support—now face direct threats over the operational zone.

---

## The Casualty Count: 11 Dead, 100 Wounded

The human cost of the guerrilla war is mounting rapidly:
*   **Killed**: 11 IDF soldiers killed since the March 2026 escalation.
*   **Wounded**: Over 48 wounded in the latest 24-hour period alone, with total casualties exceeding 100.
*   **Mercenaries**: A Greek newspaper, traditionally allied with Israel, reported that mercenary soldiers are now supporting the IDF in Lebanon—the first such report in the conflict.
*   **The Verdict**: The Greek headline read: "Everything is turning upside down—Hezbollah's fierce guerrilla war causes daily Israeli casualties."

---

## Five Pillars of the New Doctrine

Hezbollah's operational posture rests on five interconnected pillars:
*   **Long-Range Strikes**: Scud-B missiles targeting deep strategic assets like Palmachim.
*   **Small-Group Guerrilla**: Decentralized cells conducting 48+ daily operations against IDF positions.
*   **Anti-Armor Dominance**: Systematic destruction of tanks and armored vehicles using advanced ATGMs.
*   **Drone Warfare**: Kamikaze drones striking settlements beyond the Blue Line.
*   **Existential Resolve**: A doctrinal commitment to prolonged conflict, treating the war as existential and ideological rather than territorial.

---

## Conclusion: The Scud Changes Everything

The confirmed Scud-B strike on Palmachim from Lebanese territory represents a paradigm shift in the conflict.

If Hezbollah has indeed acquired Syrian ballistic missile stocks, the strategic calculus has fundamentally changed. The IDF now faces a guerrilla force that combines asymmetric ground warfare—destroying 30 tanks and killing 11 soldiers—with long-range ballistic capability previously reserved for state actors. The deployment of SAM systems against helicopters and the continuous rocket bombardment of northern settlements confirm that Hezbollah has created an operational environment where the IDF cannot achieve territorial control, air superiority, or population security simultaneously.
