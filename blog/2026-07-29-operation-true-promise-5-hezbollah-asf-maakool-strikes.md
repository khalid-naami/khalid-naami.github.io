# True Promise 5: Joint Salvos Strike Israeli Bases

The military confrontation in the Middle East has expanded into a coordinated multi-front operation. Iran launched its "True Promise 5" (الوعد الصادق 5) campaign, targeting 54 strategic installations in Israel, while Hezbollah executed its own offensive code-named "Al-Asf al-Ma'kool" (العصف المأكول). The joint operation combined heavy ballistic missiles and drone swarms, striking targets from the northern border down to central Israel.

## The Coordinated Front: True Promise 5 and Al-Asf al-Ma'kool

The alignment between Tehran and the Lebanese resistance has shifted from symbolic support to direct operational coordination. According to Israeli defense sources, the joint strike involved the launch of over 150 heavy missiles and 30 attack drones within a short window.

The IRGC confirmed the coordinated nature of the strikes, praising the effectiveness of the joint action. By launching simultaneous salvos from both western Iran and southern Lebanon, the joint front forced Israeli air defense systems to divide their tracking resources, allowing multiple projectiles to penetrate the airspace.

---

## Heavy Salvos: Sating Northern and Central Israel

The strikes targeted key cities and settlements across northern and central Israel. In the north, heavy rocket salvos struck Metula and Kiryat Shmona—the latter targeted for the third time during this phase of the escalation.

Simultaneously, the HaSharon region in central Israel experienced major explosions as heavy missiles hit industrial and logistical sites. Further south, salvos targeted Katzrin in the southern Golan Heights, alongside drone strikes in Nahariya. The volume of incoming fire activated sirens across Tel Aviv and West Jerusalem, forcing millions of residents to seek shelter.

---

## Targeting Command Centers: Dado and Stella Maris Bases

The joint operation focused on neutralizing command, control, and intelligence centers:
*   **Stella Maris Base**: Located in Haifa, this naval command facility was targeted by a swarm of suicide drones.
*   **Dado Base**: The headquarters of the IDF's Northern Command was struck by precision-guided missiles.
*   **Ein Zitim Base**: Located north of Safed, this intelligence facility was targeted by heavy salvos.
*   **Ami'ad and Shimshon Bases**: Staging grounds near the Sea of Galilee were targeted with upgraded rockets.
*   **Misgav and Tira al-Karmel Bases**: Logistical facilities in the Haifa sector suffered direct hits.

## The Six-Week War Ceiling and Trump’s Ceasefire Shift

The timing of the joint offensive has coincided with political developments in Washington. Initially, U.S. President Donald Trump indicated that a regional ceasefire was "near" during a brief interview with a journalist from Axios and Channel 12. However, following the latest strikes, the White House shifted its wording, stating that a settlement was "not soon."

This political shift occurs as the U.S. and Israel work under a negotiated six-week ceiling for the war. Fearing that the U.S. administration might withdraw support due to threats to U.S. bases in the Gulf and oil price volatility, the Israeli military has intensified its campaign, seeking to degrade Iran's capabilities before the deadline expires.

---

## Strategic Bomber Strikes on Qom: Shattering Rules of Engagement

The escalation reached a critical level following U.S. strategic bomber strikes targeting the Iranian city of Qom. U.S. heavy bombers hit civilian and industrial infrastructure in the area, signaling a shift in the campaign's parameters.

This strike has removed the remaining rules of engagement that had governed the conflict. By targeting historical and cultural centers like Qom, the coalition has expanded the scope of the war. In response, Iranian planners have standardized on heavy payloads, launching salvos against U.S. logistics nodes throughout the region.

---

## The Paris Mechanism and the Threat of European Confrontation

On the diplomatic front, French, Syrian, and Lebanese representatives have attempted to implement the "Paris Mechanism." This framework seeks to preserve Syrian neutrality by securing the Lebanese-Syrian border, ignoring international disputes over the Golan Heights.

However, Paris has issued warnings to Tel Aviv regarding the targeting of Lebanese state infrastructure. France stated that any strikes disabling Lebanon's civilian power grid or transport networks would place Israel in direct political confrontation with Europe. This warning is supported by the presence of European UNIFIL forces and German naval assets operating in the eastern Mediterranean.

---

## Conclusion: The Transition to an Unrestricted Regional War

The launch of Operation True Promise 5 and Hezbollah's Al-Asf al-Ma'kool marks a transition toward unrestricted regional warfare. By executing coordinated strikes against Israeli command centers and U.S. bases, the joint front has shown it can bypass regional air defense networks.

As the U.S. and Israel continue their bomber campaign and consider ground operations in southern Lebanon, the lack of political constraints increases the risk of a wider conflict. The presence of stealth UUV networks in the strait ensures that any attempt to bypass Iran's shipping regulations will carry a high risk of vessel loss, forcing a reassessment of naval operations in the region.
