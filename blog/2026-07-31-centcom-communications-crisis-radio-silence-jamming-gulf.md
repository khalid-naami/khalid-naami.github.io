# CENTCOM Comms Blackout: Gulf Jamming

U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) is facing a critical communications crisis in the Persian Gulf. Coordinated electronic warfare operations by the regular Iranian Army have jammed U.S. tactical communication networks, including Link 16 and Link 22 data lines. The blackout forced the activation of emergency high-frequency (HF) backup channels and Skyking networks, with alphanumeric Emergency Action Messages (EAMs) broadcast continuously to alter flights.
