# Pakistan Slams Israel: Nuclear Shield & Ceasefire

Tensions between Islamabad and Tel Aviv have reached an unprecedented peak following public declarations by Pakistan's Defence Minister, who labeled Israel "a curse on humanity" and condemned its operations in Gaza, Lebanon, and Iran. The diplomatic clash occurs as Pakistan mediates a two-stage ceasefire between Washington and Tehran. In Israel, public dissatisfaction has reached record levels, with recent polls by *Walla* and *Maariv* showing that a majority of Israelis believe the air campaign failed to secure a strategic victory.

## Pakistani Defence Minister: Israel is a Curse on Humanity

The diplomatic rhetoric from Islamabad represents a major shift in the mediation environment:
*   **The Statement**: Pakistan's Defence Minister stated that Israel represents "a curse and a threat to humanity."
*   **Targeting the Origin**: He condemned the establishment of the state on Palestinian land, linking historical grievances to the current strikes in Gaza, Lebanon, and Iran.
*   **The Context**: The comments were made while Islamabad serves as the primary diplomatic host for the U.S.-Iran ceasefire negotiations.

---

## Netanyahu's Reaction: Condemning the Islamabad Declaration

Netanyahu's office responded rapidly to the minister's statement:
*   **The Condemnation**: Tel Aviv described the statements as "shocking and unacceptable" for a government pretending to act as a neutral peace mediator.
*   **The Security Concern**: Israeli officials argued that Vance, Kushner, and Witkoff should not participate in negotiations hosted by a government expressing hostile positions.
*   **The Ultimatum**: Israel demanded that the U.S. delegation relocate the talks, a request that Washington chose to ignore.

---

## The Two-Stage Ceasefire: Structural Truce Details

The ceasefire framework negotiated in Pakistan is structured in two distinct phases:
*   **Phase 1**: An immediate 15-day halt to all direct air, missile, and drone strikes between the U.S., Israel, and the resistance axis.
*   **Phase 2**: The drafting of a comprehensive, permanent regional security agreement to be submitted to the UN Security Council.
*   **Hormuz Transit**: Phase 1 permits the daily transit of 15 commercial vessels through the Strait of Hormuz, excluding oil tankers.

---

## Electronic MOU: The Digital Agreement Channels

The initial understandings are being finalized through digital coordination channels:
*   **Electronic Signature**: The preliminary ceasefire terms are compiled into an electronic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU).
*   **Direct Routing**: Because there are no direct links between Washington, Tel Aviv, and Tehran, the document is routed electronically through Islamabad.
*   **Legal Force**: The electronic MoU serves as the binding framework until the permanent agreement is codified in New York.

---

## The Nuclear Mismatch: Pakistan and Iran Cooperation

The strategic partnership between Islamabad and Tehran is shifting the balance of power:
*   **Nuclear Umbrella**: Israel fears that Pakistan's established nuclear arsenal provides strategic cover for Iran's conventional operations.
*   **Scientific Support**: Intelligence reports suggest that technical cooperation between the two neighbors is expanding.
*   **Regional Protection**: The alignment prevents Israel from executing unilateral strikes on Pakistan's nuclear assets, securing the eastern flank of the axis.

---

## Israel Internal Polls: Walla and Maariv Disclosures

Public opinion polls in Israel indicate significant dissatisfaction with the government's military achievements:
*   **No Victory**: Polls conducted by *Walla* and *Maariv* show that 46% of Israelis believe the campaign against Iran did not achieve victory.
*   **Disapproval**: Approximately 63% of respondents expressed general dissatisfaction with the political results of the war.
*   **Opposition Split**: Dissatisfaction among opposition voters reached 83%, compared to 39% among coalition supporters.

---

## Command Trust: Air Force Lead Over Chief of Staff

The polls also revealed a gap in public trust toward different branches of the security cabinet:
*   **Air Force Trust**: The commander of the Israeli Air Force received the highest trust ratings among the public.
*   **Chief of Staff**: Trust in the IDF Chief of Staff was lower, reflecting public frustration over the prolonged ground campaign in Lebanon.
*   **Smotrich at bottom**: Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich ranked last in public trust, due to the mounting economic costs of the war.

---

## GCC Stance: Siding with Islamabad's Diplomatic Role

The Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) has adopted a cautious stance, aligning with the Pakistani mediation:
*   **Support for Peace**: The GCC issued a statement supporting Pakistan's efforts to broker the temporary ceasefire.
*   **Omission of Israel**: The official statement omitted any mention of Israel, reflecting the refusal of key members to normalize relations.
*   **Economic Interest**: The Gulf states seek a rapid stabilization of shipping lanes to protect local energy infrastructure.

---

## Conclusion: The New Regional Security Architecture

The diplomatic clash over the Pakistani Defence Minister's statements highlights the changing geopolitical landscape of West Asia.

By hosting the U.S.-Iran negotiations and proposing a two-stage electronic MoU, Islamabad has positioned itself as a central mediator, backed by its status as a nuclear power. The negative results of the *Walla* and *Maariv* polls show that Netanyahu's government faces growing domestic pressure over a war that has failed to secure a strategic victory. As the GCC aligns with the Pakistani peace initiative, the role of Israel in regional security planning is being marginalized, leaving the future of the conflict to be decided by the major powers.
