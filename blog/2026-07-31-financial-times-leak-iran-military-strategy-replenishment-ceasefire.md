# FT Leak: Iran Military Strategy & Ceasefire Arming

A cache of classified military documents obtained by the *Financial Times* has revealed Iran's long-term military strategy and its adaptation of combat lessons from the conflict in Ukraine. The leak has challenged Israeli intelligence assessments, which previously assumed Iran's industrial and command structures were degraded. Concurrently, senior IRGC Aerospace commanders have confirmed that Iran is utilizing the temporary ceasefire to replenish its missile and drone stocks at rates exceeding pre-war levels.
