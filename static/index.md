# Khalid Naami

**Founder of the Dashboard Options project** — Personal website and independent research archive covering Quantitative Finance, Options Greeks, Global Macroeconomics, Geopolitics (OSINT), and Financial AI.

## Who am I?
I am Khalid Naami, Founder of the Dashboard Options project. I publish research and quantitative analysis bridging options market microstructure (Delta, Gamma, Vanna, Charm), global macroeconomic cycles, geopolitical risk analysis, and artificial intelligence.

## Core Research Domains
- **Quantitative Finance & Options Greeks:** Gamma Exposure (GEX), Vanna, Charm, IV surfaces, and dealer hedging mechanics.
- **Global Macroeconomics:** Sovereign debt, liquidity cycles, credit markets, and central bank policy.
- **Geopolitics & OSINT:** Energy chokepoints, defense technology shifts, and strategic intermarket impacts.
- **Financial AI & Open Source:** Python, Rust, JavaScript/React data tools, and local RAG workflows.
